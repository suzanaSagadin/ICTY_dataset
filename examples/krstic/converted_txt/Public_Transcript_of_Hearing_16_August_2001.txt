 5    the accused in accordance with Rule 62 of the Rules of Procedure and

 9    Trial Chamber III.

13    language which he understands?

16            MR. VAN DER SPOEL:  I have discussed very briefly the indictment
17    with my client, and he understands it quite well.
18            JUDGE LIU:  I'm so glad to hear that.  Does he understand the
19    contents of the indictment or is he aware of the nature of the allegations
20    made against him?
21            MR. VAN DER SPOEL:  My conference with him was too brief to
22    discuss everything in detail as he sees me as his provisional Defence
23    counsel at the moment.  He was aware of his rights and it was, for him,
24    sufficient.
25            JUDGE LIU:  If you have discussed the contents of the indictment
 1    with your client, may I know whether he wishes the indictment to be read
 2    out in full, which is his right, or he wishes to waive his right?

 4            JUDGE LIU:  Thank you.  I'm very grateful to hear that.
 5            Does your client know that he must plead to the charge in the
 6    indictment within 30 days of the initial appearance, and if he so wishes,
 7    he can also do so today.  Is he ready to enter a plea today?
 8            MR. VAN DER SPOEL:  He is, Your Honour.

10    Mr. Blagojevic, are you ready to enter pleas to the charges against you in
11    the indictment?
12            THE ACCUSED: (Interpretation) I am ready.
13            JUDGE LIU:  I propose to read out to you just the charge and then
14    I'll ask you how do you plead to the charge, guilt or not guilty.  Your
15    answer in each case should be guilty or not guilty, as the case may be.
16            The first count against you is a charge of genocide.  How do you
17    plead, guilty or not guilty?
18            THE ACCUSED: (Interpretation) Your Honour, I am familiar with the
19    indictment.  I have read it and understood it, and on the basis of the
20    same, I plead not guilty on this count too.
21            JUDGE LIU:  The second count is a charge of complicity to commit
22    genocide.  How do you plead, guilty or not guilty?
23            THE ACCUSED: (Interpretation) Not guilty.
24            JUDGE LIU:  The third count is a charge of extermination, being a
25    crime against humanity.  How do you plead, guilty or not guilty?
