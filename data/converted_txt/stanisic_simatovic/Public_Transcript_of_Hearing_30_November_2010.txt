18             JUDGE ORIE:  Thank you, Madam Registrar.
19             I would first like to deliver a statement on behalf of the
20     Chamber.
21             This is the Chamber's statement with regard to the
22     contemporaneous notes of witness Miomir Kovacevic who gave evidence in
23     this case on the 27th of August, 2009, and appeared again before the
24     Chamber on the 27th of August, and the 1st and 2nd of September 2010 for
25     cross-examination.  During his cross-examination on the 1st of September,
 1     2010, the witness referred to contemporaneous notes prepared by him which
 2     he kept at home and which allegedly corroborated his evidence.  The
 3     Stanisic Defence requested that the witness provide the Chamber with
 4     these contemporaneous notes and the witness indicated on both the 1st and
 5     the 2nd of September, that he was willing to do so.
 6             This can be found at transcript pages 6745 to -47, and also at
 7     transcript pages 6824 to -25.
 8             It is the position of the Stanisic case that the witness has
 9     fabricated the existence of the contemporaneous notes and that in reality
10     they do not exist.  In this respect, the Chamber refers to transcript
11     6761.  As the parties are aware, since the time of the witness's
12     testimony, the Chamber with the assistance of the Victims and Witness
13     Section has made all possible efforts to obtain the notes from the
14     witness.  However, to date, the witness has failed to provide them.  The
15     Chamber considers that the course of events since the witness's testimony
16     may well be interpreted as an indication that the notes do not exist.
17     Under these circumstances, the Chamber has decided that it will not
18     pursue the matter further.  The Chamber will evaluate the testimony of
19     the witness without the benefit of the notes but also against the
20     background of the witness's failure to provide them.
21             If the parties would like to comment on this, they have an
22     opportunity to do so, but not today.  Mr. Jordash.
