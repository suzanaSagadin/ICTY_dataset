12             The witness is a retired JNA officer who held various positions

16             THE INTERPRETER:  Can the counsel please read slowly.  Thank you.

18             To organise the staff of the SAO Krajina TO which did not exist
19     as an institution prior to that time.
20             The witness testified that while he was in the SAO Krajina, the
21     SAO Krajina TO staff was understaffed, ill-equipped, and lacking the
22     financial resources to establish units of the Territorial Defence.  In
23     addition, the JNA and the SAO Krajina police had first priority for the
24     most able recruits which made it difficult to establish TO units.
25             While the SAO Krajina TO units were in theory subordinated to the
 1     staff of the SAO Krajina TO, in reality they were subordinated to JNA
 2     units in the area.  The term "marticevci" was used to refer to all forces
 3     of the Ministry of the Interior or MUP of the SAO Krajina.  According to
 4     the witness, as Babic was president of the SAO Krajina, in theory, he had
 5     authority over all other government employees and organisations including
 6     Martic and his Ministry of the Interior.
 7             In reality, however, Babic exerted little or no control or
 8     influence over the work of the SAO Krajina MUP.  The witness testified
 9     that Martic attended meetings with the commander of the JNA 9th corps.
10     With Martic's permission, the Krajina police participated in joint
11     operations with the JNA.  The witness also described the relations
12     between the SAO Krajina TO staff and the Ministry of Defence of Serbia
13     and testified that the SAO Krajina TO staff was totally dependent on the
14     support of the Ministry of Defence of Serbia in terms of personnel,
15     financing, and logistics.
16             The witness stated that the Ministry of the Interior of Krajina
17     had logistical support from the Republic of Serbia MUP and that this
18     included equipment with which Martic had established an excellent
19     communication system, including long-range radio stations.
20             The witness discussed the relationship between Martic and Babic
21     noting that they did not get along and could in fact never agree on
22     anything.  The witness believes that Babic established the SAO Krajina TO
23     staff to counter the influence of Martic.
24             The witness identified Jovica Stanisic and someone with the
25     nickname Frenki as members of the state security service.  During his
 1     time in the Krajina, the witness saw Martic and Frenki and the same group
