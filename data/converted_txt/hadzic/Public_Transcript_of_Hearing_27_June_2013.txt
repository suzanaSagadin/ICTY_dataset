17             Mr. Zivanovic, for the Defence.
18             MR. ZIVANOVIC:  Good morning, Your Honours.  For the Defence of
19     Goran Hadzic, Zoran Zivanovic, Christopher Gosnell, and Liane Aronchick,
20     legal assistant.
21             JUDGE DELVOIE:  Thank you very much.  I'll take a moment for a
22     oral ruling.
23             The Defence requested the admission of a number of documents used
24     to challenge the credibility of GH-026 during cross-examination on the
25     13th of June, 2013.  Documents with Rule 65 ter number 1D413, 1D416, and
 1     1D418 relate to a criminal conviction.  Documents with Rule 65 ter number
 2     1D415, 1D421, 1D423, 1D426, and 1D427 relate to a criminal investigation.
 3     The document with Rule 65 ter number 05686 consists of medical records.
 4             In view of the evidence elicited from the witness on
 5     cross-examination, the Trial Chamber does not consider it necessary the
 6     admission of further supporting material relating to the criminal
 7     investigation or medical records.  However, documents with Rule 65 ter
 8     number 1D413, 1D416, and 1D418, relating to the criminal conviction will
 9     be admitted under seal because they substantially supplement the
10     questioning during the cross-examination and will assist the
11     Trial Chamber in assessing the evidence of the witness during its final
12     deliberations in the present case.
13             Madam Registrar.
14             THE REGISTRAR:  Your Honours, those will be Exhibit numbers D75,
15     D76, and D77, under seal.
16             JUDGE DELVOIE:  Thank you.
17             THE REGISTRAR:  Your Honours, in addition to that, in an internal
18     memorandum dated 26 June 2013, 65 ter 02601 was assigned Exhibit Number
19     P2265.2186.  And 65 ter 02990 was assigned Exhibit Number P2269.2186.
20             Those are the two documents the Prosecution actually sought to
21     exclude from the 92 ter package and therefore should not have been
22     assigned numbers.
23             JUDGE DELVOIE:  Madam Registrar, could you check on the record
24     the first exhibit number.  I think the -- the number after the dot is not
25     correct.
