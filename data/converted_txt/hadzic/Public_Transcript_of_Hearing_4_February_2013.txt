17             THE REGISTRAR:  We're in open session, Your Honours.  Thank you.
18             JUDGE DELVOIE:  Thank you.
19             On January 24th, 2013, in its decision on the admission of
20     evidence pursuant to Rule 92 bis, the Trial Chamber denied the admission
21     into evidence of six documents tendered by the Prosecution.  The Chamber
22     considered that these documents were not included on the Prosecution's
23     exhibit list.  The Trial Chamber recalls, however, that it granted the
24     Prosecution leave to add these documents to its exhibit list in its
25     "Decision on Second Prosecution Motion for Leave to Amend Prosecution

 1     Rule 65 ter Exhibit List" of 19 October 2012.
 2             The Chamber further recalls its determination in the Rule 92 bis
 3     decision that:
 4             The documents with Rule 65 ter numbers 05857, 05858, 05859, and
 5     05860, form an inseparable and indispensable part of Witness GH-149's
 6     evidence; and the documents with Rule 65 ter number 06861, and 05862,
 7     form an inseparable and indispensable part of Witness GH-092's evidence.
 8             The Trial Chamber therefore considers that these six documents
 9     are appropriate for admission pursuant to Rules 89(C) and 92 bis and
10     hereby admits them into evidence.
11             In addition, the Chamber recalls that it denied admission into
12     evidence of document with Rule 65 ter number 02513 in the Rule 92 bis
13     decision.  On 25 January 2013, the Prosecution informed the Chamber that
14     it has since uploaded an English translation of the document to e-court.
15     The Defence did not object to the admission into evidence of 02513 when
16     this document was originally tendered by the Prosecution.  The Chamber
17     finds that 02513 forms an inseparable and indispensable part of
18     Witness GH-077's evidence and considers that the document is appropriate
19     for admission pursuant to Rules 89(C) and 92 bis and admits the document
20     into evidence.
21             THE INTERPRETER:  Interpreter's note, could you kindly slow down
22     for the record.  Thank you.
23             JUDGE DELVOIE:  I'm very sorry.
24             The Chamber notes that the admission of 02513 renders 02423, as
25     previously admitted, unnecessary.  The Chamber therefore orders the

 1     Prosecution to substitute 02423 with 02513.  The Registry shall mark
 2     02423 as not admitted.
 3             The Chamber instructs the Registry to take all necessary and
 4     appropriate measures to implement this decision.
 5             The third one is about the Prosecution's motion to supplement
 6     Rule 65 ter document 06231.  The Defence said they would not object to
 7     the motion on the condition that the Prosecution makes an averment as to
 8     the original date of the photographs.
 9             Has that been done, Mr. Zivanovic?  Or will it be done,
10     Mr. Stringer?
11             MR. STRINGER:  Yes, Mr. President.  I think virtually all of the
12     images have the date printed on them when the image was taken.  I think
13     there may be just one so far that does not have the date, but we will
14     make that averment at the time that we bring up that particular image.
15             JUDGE DELVOIE:  Thank you.
16             Then the motion is granted.
17             If there's nothing else, we can bring the first witness in.
18                           [Trial Chamber and Registrar confer]
