13     Mr. Hadzic to enter a plea to the operative indictment which was filed on
14     the 22nd of July, 2011.  A corrigendum to the indictment was filed on the
15     3rd of August, 2011.  The hearing is to be conducted in accordance with
16     Rule 62 of the Rules of Procedure and Evidence of this Tribunal.
17             Before we move further, I would like to inform you, Mr. Hadzic,
18     that you have a fundamental right to remain silent throughout these
19     proceedings and that it will not be held against you if you do.  Further,
20     Articles 20 and 21 of the Statute of the Tribunal guarantee you a number
21     of rights which, I recall, were read to you at Initial Appearance held on
22     the 25th of July, 2011, before Judge O-Gon Kwon.  At this
23     Initial Appearance, Judge Kwon asked you if you wished to have the
24     indictment read.  You indicated, through your counsel, that you had
25     familiarized yourself with the content of the indictment and that you did
 1     not wish to have it read aloud in court.
 2             I will ask you again today if you wish to have the indictment
 3     read aloud or whether you waive the reading of the indictment.
 4             THE ACCUSED: [Interpretation] I waive the right, Your Honour.  I
 5     do not wish to hear it.
 6             JUDGE DELVOIE:  Thank you.

 8     within the indictment.

12     contained within the indictment?

18             In due course, a date will be set for the commencement of the
19     trial, pursuant to Rule 62(A)(v).  A Status Conference in according with
20     Rule 65 bis soon will be scheduled in order to advance the preparation of
21     this case for trial as well as to allow you, Mr. Hadzic, the opportunity
22     to raise issues in relation thereto.
23             One matter that I wish to raise before we adjourn is the
24     disclosure to the Defence of the material supporting the indictment.
25     Yesterday, the 23rd of August, 2011, the Trial Chamber issued its
 1     decision on the Prosecution motion for protective measures for victims
 2     and witnesses and documentary evidence.  In this decision, the Chamber
 3     ordered, pursuant to Rule 66(A)(i), the Prosecution to disclose to the
 4     Defence by no later than the 23rd of September, 2011, the supporting
 5     material that accompanied the indictment when confirmation was sought and
 6     all prior statements obtained by the Prosecution from Mr. Hadzic.

 8     23 September to inform the Chamber and the Defence if any of the
 9     Rule 66(A)(i) material is covered by Rule 70 restrictions.
10             The Trial Chamber will issue further orders in relation to
11     additional disclosure in due course, such as required under
12     Rule 66(A)(ii).

14     misunderstandings, that pursuant to Rule 72(A), the 30-day time-limit for
15     the filing of preliminary motions will not commence until permanent
