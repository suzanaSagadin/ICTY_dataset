10             MR. GOSNELL:
11        Q.   So is it correct to say that you first learned that the
12     11 individuals who were taken away by Arkan, according to the document,
13     the first that you learned of their death was in 1995; is that correct?
14        A.   That's what I heard.  These weren't the names and surnames that I
15     was told.  All those people who disappeared, who were taken away by
16     Arkan, on the whole disappeared.  They went missing.  They were killed.
17     But in 1998 -- towards the end of 1998, and this can be seen in the
18     documents, too, or, rather, there was an exhumation that was carried out
19     and these individuals were recovered.  These bodies were recovered.
20        Q.   So does that mean that the first time that you drew a connection
21     between those names on the document and their death was, in fact, in
22     1998?
23        A.   That's correct.
24        Q.   And when you were having your conversation with Mr. Cizmic on or
25     about the 23rd or 24th of September, you didn't know that those

 1     11 individuals had been killed, correct?
 2        A.   I didn't know.
 3        Q.   Did you hear from Mr. Cizmic his view that these 11 individuals
 4     had been taken to the TO centre in Erdut and were being used by Arkan to
 5     assist in some kind of construction?
 6        A.   No, I really didn't hear anything about them.
 7        Q.   You've told us some information about the reasons that you heard
 8     for -- for the release of Mr. Sutalo and Mr. Palinkas.  Did you ever seek
 9     out Mr. Sutalo or Mr. Palinkas to obtain more details about what may have
10     happened on the evening of the 21st of September at the Zadruga detention
11     facility?  Without identifying your position at the time.
12        A.   Very well, I won't mention that.  But I had no reasons to look
13     for them and it wasn't possible for me to do so either, because they were
14     in the Republic of Croatia and we were still in the SAO Krajina.  In
15     1998, the Croatian police arrived and assumed responsibility.  They
16     returned to their own state, in fact.  So I couldn't check anything and I
17     couldn't establish contact with them either.
18        Q.   You remained in Dalj between the 24th of September and the
19     16th of October, 1991; is that correct?
20        A.   Yes.
21        Q.   And during that period of time, did you have any information
22     about the whereabouts of Mr. Sutalo or Mr. Palinkas?
23        A.   I didn't.
24        Q.   Did you ask Mr. Cizmic to --
25             MR. GOSNELL:  For this, Your Honour, I think we do need to go
