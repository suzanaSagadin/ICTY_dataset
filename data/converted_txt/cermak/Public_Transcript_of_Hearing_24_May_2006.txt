12    Conference.  Since then, of course, we have continued with our ISU
13    researching and our document reviews.  We have completed the ISU searches

15    documents.  We're just about through those 4.000 documents, but out of
16    those documents we've already made disclosure of over 200 to Defence
17    counsel.
