 20   THE REGISTRAR:   Case IT-94-1-PT, the Prosecutor against Dusko
 21   Tadic.
 22   THE PRESIDING JUDGE:  Good morning, ladies and gentlemen.
 23   Currently, there are four motions before the Trial Chamber
 24   in this case, determination of three of which, those
 25   dealing with the form of the indictment, the principle of
  1   non-bis-in-idem and a request for protective measures for
  2   a witness, have been reserved to today.  The fourth
  3   motion, which relates to the broadcasting of the
  4   Tribunal's proceedings, remains pending.
  5        The unanimous decision of the Trial Chamber on each
  6   of these three Motions is handed down today.  I shall now
  7   address each of these three Motions separately.  The first
  8   Motion is a Motion filed by the Defence on 23rd June 1995
  9   challenging the form of the indictment for being too vague
 10   and for charging offences as concurrent offences under the
 11   Statute, and not in the alternative.  Following the
 12   amendment of the indictment on September 1st 1995, a
 13   second Motion was filed on September 4th 1995.  Both
 14   Motions seek to have the indictment dismissed or,
 15   alternatively, an order that the Prosecutor further amend
 16   the indictment to rectify the alleged defects.
 17        The Prosecutor filed two Responses to those Motions,
 18   on July 7th and September 26th 1995 respectively,
 19   asserting that the indictment is not defective, that the
 20   procedures of this Tribunal as to the disclosure of
 21   evidence provide all necessary information to the accused
 22   to enable him to prepare a proper defence and that the
 23   offences may be properly charged concurrently.
 24   The hearing of the Motion was postponed pending the
 25   outcome of the interlocutory appeal on jurisdiction filed
  1   by the Defence and was eventually heard in open session on
  2   October 24th 1995.
  3   	The Order of the Trial Chamber in this matter is as
  4   follows:
  5   	Having considered the written and oral submissions of
  6   the parties, the Trial Chamber, pursuant to Rule 72 of the
  7   Rules of Procedure and Evidence:
  8   	(1) grants the Motion in so far as it relates to
  9   paragraph 4 of the indictment as amended on September 1st
 10   1995;
 11   	(2) grants leave to the Prosecutor to amend paragraph
 12   4 of the indictment within 30 days of delivery of this
 13   Decision; and
 14   	(3) dismisses the Motion in all other respects.
 15   The second Motion pending before the Trial Chamber
 16   was also filed by the Defence on June 23rd 1995.  In it,
 17   the Defence asserted that the proceedings before the Trial
 18   Chamber should be dismissed as constituting a breach of
 19   the principle of non-bis-in-idem or, as it sometimes
 20   known, double jeopardy, based on the existence of
 21   proceedings against the accused in the Federal Republic of
 22   Germany.  These proceedings before the Tribunal commenced,
 23   and the accused is present before us today, as a
 24   consequence of the deferral by the Federal Republic of
 25   Germany of its proceedings to this Tribunal pursuant to a
  1   formal request for a deferral issued by the Trial Chamber
  2   on November 8th 1994.
  3   	Again, a second Motion was filed on September 4th
  4   1995, following the amendment of the indictment, and on
  5   July 7th and September 26th 1995 the Prosecutor filed
  6   Responses contesting the Motion.  Argument was heard on
  7   this Motion, together with the Motion relating to the form
  8   of the indictment, on October 24th 1995.  At that hearing,
  9   leave was given to the Defence to file additional
 10   information concerning the nature of proceedings in the
 11   Federal Republic of Germany.  That information was filed
 12   on October 30th 1995 and the Prosecutor responded on
 13   November 1st 1995.
 14   	The Order of the Trial Chamber in this matter is as
 15   follows:
 16   	Having considered the written and oral submissions of
 17   the parties, the Trial Chamber, pursuant to Rule 72 of the
 18   Rules of Procedure and Evidence, dismisses the Motion and
 19   denies the relief sought by the Defence in its Motion on
 20   Ne-Bis-in-Idem.
 21   	The third and final Motion is one filed by the
 22   Prosecutor on July 31st 1995 seeking protective measures
 23   for a witness referred to by the letter "L".  The Motion
 24   contained 12 separate prayers for relief.  On September
 25   28th 1995 the Defence filed its Response objecting in part
  1   and agreeing in part to the protective measures sought.
  2   On October 6th 1995 the Prosecutor filed a Reply, which
  3   took into account the decision of August 10th 1995 issued
  4   by this Trial Chamber on a previous Motion by the
  5   Prosecutor seeking protective measures in respect of other
  6   witnesses, and amended the relief sought.  At the request
  7   of the Prosecutor, and with agreement of the Defence, the
  8   Motion was heard in closed session on October 25th 1995.
  9   	The Order of the Trial Chamber in this matter is as
 10   follows:
 11   	Having considered the written and oral submissions of
 12   the parties, the Trial Chamber, pursuant to Rule 75 of the
 13   Rules of Procedure and Evidence, orders as follows:
 14   	(1) the name, address, whereabouts of, and other
 15   identifying data concerning the person given pseudonym L
 16   shall not be disclosed to the public or to the media;
 17   	(2) all hearings to consider the issue of protective
 18   measures for witness L shall be in closed session;
 19   however, edited recordings and transcripts of these
 20   sessions shall be released to the public and to the media
 21   after review by the Office of the Prosecutor in
 22   consultation with the Victims and Witnesses Unit;
 23   	(3) the name, address, whereabouts of, and
 24   identifying data concerning witness L shall be sealed and
 25   not included in any of the public records of the
  1   International Tribunal;
  2   	(4) to the extent the name, address, whereabouts of,
  3   or other identifying data concerning witness L is
  4   contained in existing documents of the International
  5   Tribunal, the name and other identifying data shall be
  6   expunged from those records;
  7   	(5) documents of the International Tribunal
  8   identifying witness L shall not be disclosed to the public
  9   or to the media;
 10   	(6) the testimony of witness L shall be heard in
 11   closed session; however, edited recordings and transcripts
 12   of the session(s) shall be released to the public and to
 13   the media after review by the Office of the Prosecutor in
 14   consultation with the Victims and Witnesses Unit.
 15   	(7) the pseudonym L shall be used whenever referring
 16   to this witness in proceedings before the International
 17   Tribunal and in discussions among parties to the trial;
 18   	(8) no identifying data concerning witness L, other
 19   than his name and position at the Trnopolje camp, and the
 20   time taken for travel by witness L from his residence to
 21   the camp, shall be disclosed to Defence counsel or to the
 22   accused, and the names, addresses, whereabouts, and other
 23   identifying data concerning witness L's relatives shall
 24   not be disclosed to the Defence or to the accused.
 25   	(9) the name of witness L and information relating to
  1   the time taken for travel by witness L from his residence
  2   to the Trnopolje prison camp shall be disclosed to the
  3   Defence not less than 30 days in advance of a firm trial
  4   date;
  5   	(10) the accused, the Defence counsel and their
  6   representatives, who are acting pursuant to their
  7   instructions or request, shall not disclose the name of
  8   witness L, or any other identifying data they may discover
  9   concerning witness L, to the public or to the media,
 10   except to the limited extent such disclosure to members of
 11   the public is necessary to investigate the witness
 12   adequately.  Any such disclosure shall be made in such a
 13   way as to minimise the risk of the witness's name being
 14   divulged to the public at large or to the media;
 15   	(11) the accused, the Defence counsel and their
 16   representatives, who are acting pursuant to their
 17   instructions or request, shall notify the Office of the
 18   Prosecutor of any requested contact with witness L or the
 19   relatives of witness L, and the Office the Prosecutor
 20   shall make arrangements for such contact as is determined
 21   may be necessary; and
 22   	(12) the public and the media shall not photograph,
 23   video record or sketch witness L within the precincts of
 24   the International Tribunal.
 25   	That concludes the reading of the statement regarding
  1   these three Decisions.
