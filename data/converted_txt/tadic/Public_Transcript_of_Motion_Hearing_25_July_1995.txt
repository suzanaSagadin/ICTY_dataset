  2                customs are often defined quite vaguely and that, clearly,

 15                legality in the system in criminal law and procedure.  In

 17                punishability and competence to punish can only derive

 19                therefore, an important issue that cannot simply be taken

 21                the accused founded in human rights, the accused cannot be
 22                denied the right to challenge the establishment of the
 23                court in his case.

 11                founded upon the explicit acceptance thereof by the

 15                the jurisdiction fully examined.
