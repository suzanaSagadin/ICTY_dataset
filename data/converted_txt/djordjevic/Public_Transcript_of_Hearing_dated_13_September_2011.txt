18             This Status Conference is called in accordance with
19     Rule 65 bis (B) of the Rules of Procedure and Evidence of the Tribunal,
20     which requires a Status Conference to be convened every 120 days during
21     the appeal proceedings to allow that any person in custody pending appeal
22     the opportunity to raise issues in relation thereto, including the mental
23     and physical condition of that person.
24             In the present case, the last Status Conference was held on the

10             Now I will recall the recent procedural history of this case.
11     Before we turn to any issues that parties may wish to raise, I would like
12     to briefly note a few recent procedural matters.
13             At the last Status Conference, I indicated that the deadline for
14     any party wishing to file a motion to vary their grounds of appeal
15     following the translation of the trial judgement into B/C/S would expire
16     on the 14th of June, 2011.  Mr. Lukic was the only party to file such
17     motion within the prescribed time limit.  On the 9th September 2011, the
18     Appeals Chamber dismissed his re-filed motion requesting leave to vary
19     his notice of appeal and the appeal brief.  Accordingly, the translation
20     of the trial judgement into B/C/S will no longer constitute good cause
21     for any party seeking to vary their grounds of appeal under Rule 108 of
22     the Rules.  There are currently no pending motions in the present case.
