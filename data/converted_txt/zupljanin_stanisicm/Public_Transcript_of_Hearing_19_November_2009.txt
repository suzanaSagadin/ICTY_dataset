24             THE REGISTRAR:  We are in open session, Your Honours.
25             JUDGE HALL:  This matter, Ms. Korner, of the videolink witnesses
 1     for January.  The e-mail that you would have sent yesterday as to moving
 2     those witnesses to the week following when we expected them, we just need
 3     to have that formalised in a motion by you that the -- so that the
 4     necessary administrative --
 5             MS. KORNER:  You mean, it simply won't do for us to arrange it?
 6     We have actually got to file a motion to deal with that?
 7             JUDGE HALL:  It can be done orally, certainly.
 8             MS. KORNER:  I thought we had done that orally.  Oh, I see.  Oh,
 9     right.
10             Well, then, Your Honours, we formally apply to move the videolink
11     witnesses from the week that we had arranged, which I think was the week
12     of the 13th of January, to the following week.
13             JUDGE HALL:  So ordered.
14             JUDGE DELVOIE:  Ms. Korner, as from the planning for next week,
15     you gave us a series of witnesses, and now you are saying that
16     Mr. Trbojevic is coming the week after.
17             MS. KORNER:  Yes, that's what I told you this morning.  I just
18     gave his --
19             JUDGE DELVOIE:  Yeah, what day?  Monday?
20             MS. KORNER:  He is coming on the -- Tuesday, he will start
21     testifying on Tuesday, the 1st of December.
22             JUDGE DELVOIE:  Because the way I looked into the planning and
23     the estimate times, the witnesses you were speaking about this morning
24     will take more or less two weeks.
25             MS. KORNER:  No.  Your Honours, the witness who will start
 1     tomorrow is 92 ter and will finish on Monday, we estimate.
 2             JUDGE DELVOIE:  Yes.  Well, my calculation was that you would
 3     finish ST-113.
 4             MS. KORNER:  113 is one hour in chief, and two hours
 5     cross-examination all together.  So if does -- he is -- [overlapping
 6     speakers]... cross tomorrow.  The next one ST-121 who is viva voce is
 7     four hours in chief, three and a half in cross.  So that's seven and a
 8     half hours.  That's effectively --
 9             JUDGE DELVOIE:  Ms. Korner, this is not the way we -- because you
10     can't follow this orally.  Should we exchange the data we have on those?
11     I'll ask the ALO to send you a mail.
12             MS. KORNER:  Yes, we'll do that.  And Your Honours, may I just
13     mention in passing very quickly, Your Honours, may feel in the light of
14     what arose this morning that it may not be a bad idea to have a 65 ter
15     conference at some stage to deal with where we are going effectively, and
16     what we have to do if we are going to have a problem over adjudicated
17     facts in particular.  But there are other matters I still think, which at
18     some stage perhaps we might deal with in such a conference.  It's just a
19     suggestion for Your Honours to consider.
20             JUDGE HALL:  I think we, ourselves, had thought so, Ms. Korner.
