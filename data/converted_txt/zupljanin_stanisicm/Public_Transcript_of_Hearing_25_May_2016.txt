14             This Status Conference is called in accordance with Rule 65 bis
15     of the Rules of Procedure and Evidence of this Tribunal.  65 bis (B)
16     requires a Status Conference to be convened within 120 days after the
17     filing of a Notice of Appeal and, thereafter, within 120 days after the
18     last Status Conference.  This is to allow any person in custody pending
19     the appeal the opportunity to raise issues in relation thereto, including
20     the mental and physical condition of that person.
21             In the present case, the parties filed their notices of appeal on
22     the 13th of May, 2013, and the first Status Conference was held on
23     4th September, 2013.  The second to ninth Status Conferences were held on
24     11th of December, 9th April, 24th July, and 12th November, 9th March,
25     30th June, and 15th October of 2015, and 10th February 2016,
 1     respectively.  Today's Status Conference is the tenth in this case and
 2     was scheduled pursuant to an order issued on 21st April, 2016.
