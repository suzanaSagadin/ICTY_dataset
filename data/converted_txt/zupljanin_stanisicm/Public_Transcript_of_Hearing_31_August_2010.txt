 1             Judge Delvoie.
 2             JUDGE DELVOIE:  Thank you.
 3             The Chamber is seized of the Prosecution's request for leave to
 4     amend its Rule 65 ter summary for Witness ST-195, filed confidentially on
 5     the 23rd of August, 2010.  The Prosecution submits that when it
 6     interviewed ST-195 after the filing of the 65 ter summary, it found out
 7     he could testify on other relevant issues inter alia the structural
 8     hierarchy of communications between SJB Skender Vakuf and CSB Banja Luka
 9     and meetings attended by the accused Zupljanin hereinafter collectively
10     called "issues."
11             The Defence filed a confidential joint response on the
12     26th of August opposing the Prosecution's request.  The Chamber considers
13     the issues prima facie relevant.  Furthermore, they do not expand or
14     modify the case against the accused.  The Chamber notes, however that the
15     Prosecution filed its 65 ter summary for ST-195 on the 8th of June, 2009,
16     based on his statement and testimony before the BiH authorities.  It
17     interviewed him and got his statement only on the 8th and
18     9th of December, 2009.  This request should have been filed sooner and
19     not a mere week before ST-195's scheduled testimony.
20             Nonetheless, despite the fact that the Prosecution has not shown
21     due diligence, the Chamber finds that by disclosing ST-195's Prosecution
22     statement, including the issues to Defence on the 3rd of February, 2010,
23     the Defence have been on notice.  The Chamber notes that this Prosecution
24     statement has been available to the Defence for seven months.  The
25     Chamber is therefore not persuaded that -- by the Defence submission that
 1     granting the Prosecution's request will prejudice the accused's right to
 2     a fair trial because the Defence lacks the time and facilities to conduct
 3     their own investigations surrounding the issues.
 4             For the foregoing reasons and in the interests of justice, the
 5     Chamber hereby grants the request and allows the amendment to the
 6     65 ter summary of ST-195.
 7             JUDGE HALL:  Thank you, Judge Delvoie.
