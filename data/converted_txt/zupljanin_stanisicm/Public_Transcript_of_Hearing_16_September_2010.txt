10     May we have the appearances, please.

15     absent.
16             MR. HANNIS:  And for the Prosecution, Your Honours, I'm
17     Thomas Hannis, along with Matthew Olmsted and Crispian Smith.
18             MR. O'SULLIVAN:  Your Honour, Eugene O'Sullivan and
19     Deirdre Montgomery for Mr. Stanisic.
20             MR. KRGOVIC:  Good morning, Your Honours.  Dragan Krgovic,
21     Igor Pantelic, and Aleksandar Aleksic for Zupljanin Defence.
22             JUDGE HALL:  Thank you.
23             Before we go into closed session, the Chamber has an oral ruling.
24             The Trial Chamber is seized of a Prosecution motion -- and I
25     interrupt myself to say that there's a possibility the interpreters may
 1     not have this, so I will speak as slowly as possible.
 2             The Trial Chamber is seized of a Prosecution motion filed on the
 3     14th of September, 2010, to amend the 65 ter list of exhibits by
 4     reinstating one document, Rule 65 ter number 505, a dispatch from
 5     CSB Banja Luka, dated the 4th of August, 1992, signed by the
 6     Accused Zupljanin.  Yesterday, the Defence teams objected and the
 7     Prosecution replied orally.
 8             The Chamber is satisfied of the prima facie relevance and
 9     probative value of the document.  Given that the document was disclosed
10     to the Stanisic and Zupljanin Defence teams on the 13th of June, 2005,
11     and the 14th of October, 2008, respectively, and until May of 2008, it
12     was on the 65 ter list, it finds that the Defence have been on sufficient
13     notice and that no prejudice will be suffered by the Defence by adding
14     this half-page document to the Prosecution's 65 ter list of exhibits.
15             For these reasons and in the interests of justice, the Chamber
16     allows the addition of Rule 65 ter number 505 to the Prosecution's
17     exhibit list.
18             Thank you.  And we may now go back into closed session.
