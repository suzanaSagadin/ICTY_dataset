 1                          Friday, 17 December 2004
 2                          [Status Conference]
 3                          [Open session]
 4                          [The appellant entered court]
 5                          --- Upon commencing at 4.30 p.m.
 6            JUDGE MUMBA:  Mr. Registrar, please call the case.
 7            THE REGISTRAR:  Good afternoon, Your Honour.  This is Case Number
 8    IT-03-72-A, The Prosecutor versus Milan Babic.
 9            JUDGE MUMBA:  May I please have appearances, Prosecution, please.
10            MR. McKEON:  Yes, Your Honour.  Senior trial attorney Mark McKeon
11    for the Prosecution, and I am at counsel table with legal officer, Xavier
12    Tracol, and our case manager, Susan Grogan.
13            JUDGE MUMBA:  Thank you.  Defence, please.
14            MR. MUELLER:  Good afternoon, Your Honour.  Peter Michael Mueller
15    from Munich, lead counsel for Mr. Babic, and Mr. Robert Fogelnest from New
16    York as co-counsel.  May I ask you a favour, Madam, please.  Would you
17    permit me to wear my sunglasses.  I underwent a laser operation on my left
18    eye.  It would feel much more comfortable if I could wear it.
19            JUDGE MUMBA:  Yes, please.  We need counsel's eyes sharp.  You may
20    go ahead.
21            MR. MUELLER:  Thank you very much.
22            JUDGE MUMBA:  May I ask Mr. Milan Babic whether he can understand
23    the proceedings.
24            THE APPELLANT: [Interpretation] Yes, Your Honour.  Thank you.
25            JUDGE MUMBA:  Thank you, please sit down.
 1            These proceedings are convened according to Rule 65 bis,
 2    subparagraph (b).  It is the first Status Conference since the notice of
 3    appeal was filed by the appellant.  And the purpose of the Status
 4    Conference is to find out any matters concerning the health of the
 5    appellant and the matters of detention pertaining to the appellant and any
 6    other matters that the parties may raise.  I note that the filings are
 7    going on on time so far.  We've had the notice of appeal and the brief,
 8    and the other pleadings will follow according to the Rules as the parties
 9    are aware.  And as far as motions are concerned, we have none which are
10    pending.
11            I would like to ask Mr. Babic right away, the appellant, whether
12    he has any issues he wishes to raise regarding his health.
13            THE APPELLANT: [Interpretation] Thank you, Your Honour.  As far as
14    my health is concerned, my health is under control.  I have treatment for
15    my chronic illnesses.  As for my other issues, I would like to address you
16    in private session regarding that.
17            JUDGE MUMBA:  That is about health.  Any other matters regarding
18    your status in detention, the facilities or any other matters you wish to
19    raise?
20            THE APPELLANT: [Interpretation] Yes, Your Honour.  But could we
21    please turn into private session for that.
22            JUDGE MUMBA:  Counsel for the Defence, is that in agreement with
23    your instructions that we go into private session since this is a public
24    hearing.
25            MR. MUELLER:  I know.  I was about to stand up and to ask you for
 1    a favour to give me the floor.
 2            JUDGE MUMBA:  Yes.  Mr. Babic can sit down as counsel is on his
 3    feet.
 4            MR. MUELLER:  Mr. Babic is probably making some comments on his,
 5    let's say, psychological situation.
 6            JUDGE MUMBA:  Yes.
 7            MR. MUELLER:  In particular, he's going to address the situation
 8    of his family.
 9            JUDGE MUMBA:  Yes.
10            MR. MUELLER:  He kindly asked me to ask you for closed session.
11    As you know, there is a particular problem perhaps if we do all this in
12    open session.
13            JUDGE MUMBA:  I see.  All right.  I would like to ask the
14    Prosecution counsel whether there are any objections.
15            MR. McKEON:  No, there's no objection, Your Honour.  I think it
16    would be appropriate to have this portion of the hearing in a private
17    session.
18            JUDGE MUMBA:  Very well, thank you.
19            May I ask the Registrar, please, to ask the relevant office for
20    private session.
