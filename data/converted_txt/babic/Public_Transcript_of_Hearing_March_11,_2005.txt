 2             This is a Status Conference under the provisions of Rule 65 bis,
 3     paragraph B, where a Chamber is required to hold a Status Conference for
 4     any person who is in detention, in this case awaiting an appeal.  This is
 5     to allow the appellant to express concerns relating to the appeal or the
 6     detention conditions or the health of the appellant, and also to update
 7     the appellant with respect to the status of the appeal.
 8             The last Status Conference was held on the 17th of December,
 9     2004.  We are holding this Status Conference a little too earlier simply
10     because the appeal is ready for hearing and the scheduling order has
11     already been issued and the appeal hearing will be held on the 25th of
12     April this year in the morning, has the parties have already been
13     informed.
14             I would like to find out from Mr. Babic as to whether he has
15     concerns that you would like to raise.
16             Before he does that, I would like to inform him that following
17     the matters that were raised during the last Status Conference corning
18     his family, the Appeals Chamber did request the registrar to file a
19     report on the status of the family of the appellant and the registrar did
20     on the 3rd of March file the report.  So that the Appeals Chamber Judges
21     are aware of what is happening.  The report so filed is under
22     confidential cover so it can be discussed and there is no need to discuss
23     it except to inform the appellant that the Appeals Chamber is aware of
24     what is going on.
