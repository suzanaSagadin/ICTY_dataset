 4     conference of the 4th of July.  So these are a few extra days.
 5             We need to be able to organise these hearings, if it is necessary
 6     to organise more than one.  How many witnesses had you planned to hear,
 7     Ms. Isailovic?
 8             MS. ISAILOVIC: [Interpretation] Your Honour, at the status
 9     conference which was held on the 4th of July, if I remember correctly, I
10     accepted this deadline.  I received this information through
11     Mr. Guy-Smith.  I felt that this was a reasonable deadline, in light of
12     this case, but I would like to share with you a number of difficulties
13     I've come up against.  And for very trivial reasons, I must tell you that
14     it has to do with the time of year we are in; namely, the summer recess.
15             I spent my holiday in Belgrade.  I cut my holiday short.  I was
16     on a holiday with my parents in Belgrade, because I needed to attend this
17     status conference today.  I have tried to contact a number of people
18     whilst I was on holiday, because lawyers are working all the time.  So
19     I've come up against this difficulty because all the people I try to
20     contact are on holiday at the moment, and this will be the case until the
21     15th of August, which makes things a little bit trying for me.

23     postpone this to the beginning of September, the first week in September
24     perhaps.

 1     on the 2nd of September.  That is the only day I will not be able to come
 2     here.

 4     members of the Bench, because we had agreed on this date of the 26th of

 6     postpone the date.

 8     concerned, I must tell you that this proceeding will be a fairly simple
 9     one.  It shouldn't last more than a day.

11             MS. ISAILOVIC: [Interpretation] This, of course, will depend on
12     the availability of the people I would like to call as witnesses.

14     to call?

17     the other people are concerned, I have -- I intend to call three other
18     witnesses, and this should be fairly quick, since I shall focus on some
19     very limited issues.
20             I will have a number of documents.  I believe that in the
21     contempt of the Tribunal case, we cannot use the e-court system, can we?
22             JUDGE LATTANZI: [Interpretation] How many documents do you intend
23     to use?
24             MS. ISAILOVIC: [Interpretation] For the time being, I'm not quite
25     sure.  Something ranging from -- of course, it would depend on the length
 1     of the document in question, but I will try to be as accurate as
 2     possible.

 4     how difficult it is for the CLSS to do all of this.  This is why I have
 5     asked to have all the documents that have been translated already into
 6     the official languages of the Tribunal, and for the remaining documents,
 7     I believe I'm going to have them translated in Belgrade.  This concerns
 8     just a few documents.  Prior to that, I nonetheless shall address the
 9     translation services of this Tribunal.  But I believe it would be
10     impossible to have this translated by the Tribunal in such a short space
11     of time.  That accounts for 20 or so documents, but these are not very
12     large documents.
13             JUDGE LATTANZI: [Interpretation] Fine.

15     do-able in one day.  The maybe I could ask to have an extra hour or so,
16     but by and large that's what it would be.

19     release request filed by the accused, since, and let me quote "the weight

21     light of the circumstances of the case," and the guarantees provided by
22     the Republic of Serbia do not guarantee to the Tribunal that this person
23     will return to the UNDU if he were to be provisionally released, so the
24     Trial Chamber has rejected this request.
