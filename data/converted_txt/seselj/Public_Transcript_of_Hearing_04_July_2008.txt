 1                           Friday, 4 July 2008
 2                           [Status Conference]
 3                           [Open session]
 4                           [The accused entered court]
 5                           --- Upon commencing at 2.29 p.m.
 6             JUDGE LATTANZI: [Interpretation] Good afternoon to everyone in
 7     the courtroom.
 8             Madam Registrar, please call the case.  Thank you.
 9             THE REGISTRAR:  Good afternoon, Your Honour.  This is case number
10     IT-03-67-R77.1-PT, contempt case against Ljubisa Petkovic.
11             JUDGE LATTANZI: [Interpretation] I'd like to welcome the accused
12     as well as his temporarily assigned counsel, Ms. Branislava Isailovic.
13     Welcome to you.
14             MS. ISAILOVIC: [Interpretation] Good afternoon, Your Honour.
15     Thank you.
16             JUDGE LATTANZI: [Interpretation] Do we have technical problems?
17     I can see that there is a problem, but it has been solved.
18             Mr. Petkovic, can you hear the translation of what is being said
19     in the courtroom in a language which you understand?
20             THE ACCUSED: [Interpretation] Apologies.  Yes, I do have -- I am
21     receiving interpretation.
22             JUDGE LATTANZI: [Interpretation] Pursuant to Rule 65 bis (A) of
23     the Rules, a Trial Chamber or a Trial Chamber Judge shall convene a
24     Status Conference within 120 days of the initial appearance of the
25     accused, and its aim is to organize exchanges between the parties so as
 1     to ensure expeditious preparation for trial.  I'd like to add that it is
 2     in the interests of justice to do our utmost so that this trial can be
 3     conducted as soon as possible.
 4             This Status Conference also gives an opportunity to review the
 5     status of the case and to allow the accused the opportunity to raise
 6     issues thereto, including his health condition.
 7             Are you listening to me, Mr. Petkovic?  Is everything so far
 8     clear?
 9             THE ACCUSED: [Interpretation] Yes, Judge Lattanzi, it is.
10             JUDGE LATTANZI: [Interpretation] Given that Mr. Petkovic is
11     charged with only one count, it was deemed appropriate to convene this
12     Status Conference well before the expiry of the dead-line, and right
13     after the assignment of Ms. Isailovic by the Registrar.  We have so
14     decided in order to conduct a very expeditious trial.
15             I'd like to turn to the Defence counsel.  Ms. Isailovic, have you
16     received all the materials which had been disclosed to the duty counsel
17     in this case?  Have you received everything so far?
18             MS. ISAILOVIC: [Interpretation] Thank you, Your Honour.  I
19     believe I have received almost everything from my predecessor,
20     Mr. Guy-Smith.  I have received the materials that were disclosed to him
21     upon his the request by the Prosecutor.  I have received all these
22     materials.  But given the fact that I was assigned very recently, I was
23     in Paris yesterday, I was very busy because of the upcoming vacation.
24     However, while I was in the train, I have managed to review all the
25     materials, and it seems to me that the latest summons was dated -- which
 1     was dated end of April was not amongst the materials that were given to
 2     me.
 3             My client has disclosed his case-related materials, but they're
 4     drafted in Serbian but I would like to have them in French.
 5             JUDGE LATTANZI: [Interpretation] Yes.  Because it's working
 6     language at the Tribunal, therefore, you'd like to have them in French.
 7     Very well.
 8             MS. ISAILOVIC: [Interpretation] So this latest summons dated
 9     April 2008, as well as the notification of delivery, is missed from my
10     file.  I don't have them.  But in due time, I will turn to the Prosecutor
11     or the registry to have them.
12             JUDGE LATTANZI: [Interpretation] We will do our best to make sure
13     that you receive those materials in the afternoon.  After having received
14     these materials, do you think that disclosure will be completed?
15             MS. ISAILOVIC: [Interpretation] Yes, Your Honour.  I believe that
16     after I've received those materials, disclosure will be completed.  I
17     would like to inform you that I intend to file a motion for provisional
18     release as soon as possible.  I have seen the dates for court recess.
19     Court recess is supposed to start on the 25th of July?
20             JUDGE LATTANZI: [Interpretation] That's right.
21             MS. ISAILOVIC: [Interpretation] And court recess will finish
22     mid-August, so I intend to file this motion as soon as possible, possibly
23     by Monday.
24             JUDGE LATTANZI: [Interpretation] Very well.  I must say that I'm
25     very happy that you're speaking French.  This makes life easier for me.
 1             During the initial appearance, we were informed by the duty
 2     counsel that 45 days might be necessary for the preparation of this case.
 3     What is your position in that regard?
 4             MS. ISAILOVIC: [Interpretation] Your Honour, I haven't received
 5     that information, maybe my colleague forgot to mention it, but it seems
 6     to me that this is a reasonable dead-line.  I've had experience with
 7     another contempt case before this Tribunal, and it seems appropriate.
 8     The proceedings in contempt cases are not as complex in -- as in other
 9     cases.
10             JUDGE LATTANZI: [Interpretation] Therefore, you confirmed that
11     this time-period will be sufficient for the preparation of this case.
12     Very well.
13             Can you already give us an indication as to the number of
14     witnesses that you intend to call, even though it might be a little too
15     early.  But maybe you already know how many witnesses you intend to call,
16     as well as the number of courtroom hours which you wish to use in this
17     case?
18             MS. ISAILOVIC: [Interpretation] Your Honour, it might be a little
19     presumptuous to give you an indication now, given the fact that I was
20     assigned to this case very recently.  However, in view of my experience
21     in similar cases, I believe that a few witnesses, between three and five
22     witnesses, would be called.
23             For the time being, I don't know exactly what the Defence case
24     will be.  Not everything has been decided.  So maybe I can tell you at a
25     later stage how many exhibits, how many witnesses will be used.  However,
 1     I can tell you already that I will do my best to be brief.  I'll be
 2     brief.  I was temporarily assigned to this case, but we have to act
 3     quickly.
 4             JUDGE LATTANZI: [Interpretation] Yes.  We have to do our utmost
 5     in order to conduct an expeditious trial.
 6             MS. ISAILOVIC: [Interpretation] Yes, I agree with you.  In the
 7     course of July, I think we'll be able to tell you more precisely what we
 8     intend to do.
 9             JUDGE LATTANZI: [Interpretation] At any rate, you know how to
10     contact the Legal Officer of the Chamber.  If need be, you can contact
11     her.  You can ask her the necessary information at the end of this
12     hearing.
13             MS. ISAILOVIC: [Interpretation] Thank you, Your Honour.  I think
14     this will be very useful.  I believe that we will cooperate in a very
15     effective manner.
16             JUDGE LATTANZI: [Interpretation] Mr. Petkovic, do you have any
17     issues to raise as to the conditions of your detention?
18             THE ACCUSED: [Interpretation] Honourable Judge Lattanzi, I have
19     never been in prison or in detention so far, and I cannot tell what the
20     situation in other detention units is.
21             As for the Detention Unit I'm presently -- I'm currently in, I
22     can only say things that are commendable for the UNDU staff.
23             I don't know what else I could say.
24             JUDGE LATTANZI: [Interpretation] Thank you.
25             Could we move into private session for a few minutes.

 5     anything to add?
 6             MS. ISAILOVIC: [Interpretation] I thank you, Your Honour.  I
 7     believe that for now all the topics on the agenda have been dealt with.

 9     that I was assigned provisionally.  I explained to Mr. Petkovic and I

11     procedure can be very short, before actually the registry checks the
12     situation of the other counsel.
13             I believe that Mr. Petkovic understood this and that he now

24     approach?

 1     certain that we will complete all the necessary formalities during next
 2     week.

 4             Mrs. Isailovic, I wanted you to know that you will be kept
 5     informed in due time of other Status Conferences that might be organized
 6     if need be.
 7             MS. ISAILOVIC: [Interpretation] Thank you, Your Honour, for this.
 8     I apologise.  I'd forgotten to turn on the microphone.
 9             I took note of what you just told me.

12     adjourn this hearing.  I'd like to thank everyone and see you next time.
