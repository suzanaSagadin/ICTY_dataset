 5     forbade Nemanja Sarovic from coming?

18     view on the facts of this case.
19             THE ACCUSED: [Interpretation] Mr. Trechsel, you're going to do

 1     The Hague Tribunal.  Ten years ago Zoran Djindjic, the Mafia
 2     prime minister of Serbia asked Carla del Ponte the following:  Take him

 5     elementary procedural rights, and I'm not going to give in one iota.  I
 6     haven't got a legal advisor.  I haven't got a Case Manager.  No
 7     proceedings.  Actually, there can be proceedings.  You can finish this

10     denied a Case Manager, a legal advisor, and then everything will be done.
11             JUDGE TRECHSEL:  As you have said so yourself now, it is, in

13     note that the administration of evidence phase of the trial is over.  You
14     will not present any factual submissions on this case, which is
15     absolutely your right.  It means that we come to the last stage of the
16     trial -- do you want to?
17             I'm sorry, my colleague wants to intervene.
18                           [Trial Chamber confers]
19             JUDGE TRECHSEL:  I am correctly advised that I should state what
20     you know and we know, namely, that all the material germane to this

22     need of any further documents.

24     submissions.  And you have the opportunity, Mr. Seselj, to speak and to
25     state what you want to state for your Defence.  You have the floor.
 1             THE ACCUSED: [Interpretation] These are third contempt
 2     proceedings against me.  In the first two proceedings, although these

 4     Canada, from the state of Manitoba.  You involved him in the prosecution,
 5     and then that even wasn't sufficient.  Then he also had an assistant who
 6     had the right to address the Court directly.  And then when the Iceland
 7     volcano erupted, then that was an excuse for that amicus curiae not to
 8     come from Manitoba.
 9             So you are highly partial.  You are not objective at all.  You
10     are not providing me with elementary things.  Fifteen Prosecutors
11     appeared in these proceedings and who knows how many assistants.  I
12     always sit here on my own.  Well, now I don't want to sit here on my own.
13     I have the right to have my legal advisor here in the courtroom with me,
14     Dejan Mirovic LL.M., and my Case Manager, Nemanja Sarovic, who also has a
15     degree in law.
16             In these proceedings, I did not ask you to pay for the
17     professional services they provide to me but you have to pay their travel
18     expenses.  Now, this is the problem here.  The Registry is refusing to
19     register Nemanja Sarovic as Case Manager.  Nemanja Sarovic is the
20     Case Manager in the main proceedings.  He was also Case Manager in the
21     previous contempt proceedings.  Now, all of a sudden, the Registry does
22     not want to have him registered.  Well, that cannot be done that way and
23     I'm going to prove it to you that you cannot act that way and you do
24     whatever you want.
25             However, you are violating procedural law terribly, and I believe
 1     that all of this works against the authority of The Hague Tribunal, the
 2     professional authority of The Hague Tribunal.  And, basically, my
 3     fundamental objective is to prove that you are in a legal court and not a
 4     single honourable and honest man ever agreed to act as a judge in this
 5     Court.
 6             I said that to you last time and you were flabbergasted, but that
 7     is a generally known fact and everyone in the world knows this.  Which
 8     honest and honourable person would agree to be in your shoes?  No one,
 9     especially not in the shoes of Mr. Kwon, who signed a false indictment
10     against me.  And then when as Vice-President of the Tribunal he was
11     supposed to take a decision on the recusal of another Judge, he recused
12     himself because he appeared once in the pre-trial stage.  However, that
13     did not prevent him from appearing in three contempt proceedings and
14     pretending to be a Judge.
15             JUDGE TRECHSEL:  Please proceed.
16             THE ACCUSED: [Interpretation] Why would I?  There is no need for
17     me to proceed.  As far as I'm concerned, you can state your verdict
18     straight away.  What I have to say, I'm going to say in the appeal.  You
19     have prevented me from mounting a Defence.  I just added to the
20     procedural objections I had, and I have not even touched on the merits of
21     this case.  You prevented me from doing so.  The fact that I am self
22     represented does not mean that I have to appear totally by myself in this
23     courtroom.  I am entitled to professional and expert assistance and also
24     I'm entitled to technical assistance.

 1     Mr. Seselj, or is there anything you would like to add?

 7     the documents translated.
