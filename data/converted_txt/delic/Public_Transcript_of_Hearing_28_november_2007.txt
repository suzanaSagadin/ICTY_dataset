15            JUDGE MOLOTO:  Thank you very much.
16            And for the Defence.
17            MS. VIDOVIC: [Interpretation] Good morning, Your Honours.  Good
18    morning to my learned friends from the Prosecution.  Vasvija Vidovic and
19    Nicholas Robson appearing on behalf of General Rasim Delic, together with
20    our legal assistant, Lana Deljkic.

22            One or two things, perhaps, before we call the witness.

24                          [Trial Chamber confers]
25            JUDGE MOLOTO:  But before we do that, I just wanted to say that we
 1    are still continuing pursuant to Rule 15 bis because, again, Judge Harhoff
 2    is not available.  He's in another case at the moment.
 3            The Trial Chamber will now render its proprio motu decision on the
 4    admission into evidence of the record of the site visit conducted by the
 5    Trial Chamber between the 3rd and the 6th September 2007, pursuant to Rule
 6    4 of the Rules of Procedure and Evidence.
 7            The Trial Chamber notes that the audio-visual record has been
 8    recorded on two DVD discs.  Those discs, as well as transcripts thereof,
 9    have been provided to the parties and the Bench by the Registry.  The
10    audio-visual record and the transcripts are relevant and have probative
11    value in the present trial.
12            Therefore, pursuant to Rules 4, 54, and 89(C) of the Rules of
13    Procedure and Evidence, the Trial Chamber admits them as exhibits of the
14    Trial Chamber.  The Registrar is requested to assign an exhibit number to
15    the two DVD discs and a separate exhibit number to the transcripts.
16            Thank you very much.
17            THE REGISTRAR:  Your Honours, in light of the oral decision just
18    rendered, the DVD discs will be Exhibit 889 and the record of the
19    transcripts will be Exhibit number 890.
20            JUDGE MOLOTO:  And 889 is for the two discs?
21            THE REGISTRAR:  The two DVD discs, yes.
22            JUDGE MOLOTO:  Thank you very much.
23            And the transcript, 890?
24            THE REGISTRAR:  That's correct, Your Honour.
25            JUDGE MOLOTO:  Thank you very much.
