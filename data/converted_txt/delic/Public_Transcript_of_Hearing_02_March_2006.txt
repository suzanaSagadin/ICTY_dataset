14            MS. WHITAKER:  Good morning, Your Honour, I'm Quincy Whitaker,

18            The Chamber is currently seized of the Prosecution motion

 6    going to file its proposed amended indictment yet again and make
 7    determination on the -- any challenges to the indictment altogether.  I

 9            MR. MUNDIS:  Thank you, Mr. President, Your Honour.  That would

16    there is anything for the parties to raise.

18    Your Honour.
