15             Pursuant to paragraph 20(A)(ii) of the remuneration scheme for

17     the scheme of 1 April 2010, legal associates have no right of audience
18     before the Court unless the Chamber seized of the case decides otherwise.
19             The Chamber seized of the Tolimir appeal is the Appeals Chamber
20     and Rule 65 bis (B) of the Tribunal's Rules of Procedure and Evidence
21     gives me authority to deal with issues arising in the context of Status
22     Conferences, including matters such as courtroom presence and the right
23     of audience during Status Conferences.
24             Considering that Mr. Gajic has served as legal associate to
25     Mr. Tolimir during the trial proceedings and that he's involved in the
 1     present appeal, I am satisfied that granting Mr. Gajic a right of
 2     audience limited to addressing legal or administrative issues during
 3     Status Conferences will be in the interests of justice.
 4             However, if you, Mr. Tolimir, wish that Mr. Gajic be granted a
 5     right of audience in the appeal proceedings beyond addressing the
 6     Pre-appeal Judge at Status Conferences, you should submit a written
 7     request to the full Bench of the Appeals Chamber.
 8             Mr. Gajic, you may remain in the courtroom today.
 9             As you are aware, Rule 65 bis (B) of the Rules of Procedure and
10     Evidence requires a Status Conference to be convened within 120 days
11     after the filing of a Notice of Appeal and, thereafter, within 120 days
12     after the last Status Conference for each appellant in the Tribunal's
13     custody.
14             A Status Conference serves two primary purposes.  First, it
15     allows the Appellants an opportunity to express concerns relating to
16     their appeal or detention conditions.  Second, it provides an opportunity
17     to update the appellants with respect to the status of their case.
18             The Status Conference is also an opportunity for the Tribunal to
19     inquire about the mental and physical condition of the detained persons.
20     In the present case, the Notice of Appeal was filed on 11 March 2013.
21     Thus, the Status Conference is taking place before the expiration of the
22     120-day period specified in Rule 65 bis (B).  Today's Status Conference
23     was scheduled by the Scheduling Order issued on 17 June 2013.
