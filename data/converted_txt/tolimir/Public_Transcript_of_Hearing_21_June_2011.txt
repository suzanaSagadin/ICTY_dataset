25             In early June 1992, then-Captain First Class Savcic joined the
 1     VRS.  He was assigned as a chief -- as the Chief of Staff of the

 5     Lieutenant-Colonel Jovo Jazic.  During Commander Savcic's hospitalisation

 7     commanded the regiment.
 8             The main tasks of the 65th Protection Regiment were providing
 9     security for the Main Staff command posts, ensuring safe work conditions
10     for the organ of the Main Staff command -- organs of the Main Staff
11     command and securing high-level officers and carrying out other orders of
12     the superior command.

14     morale, religious and legal affairs, and for logistics, in addition to
15     Savcic and Jazic.  The establishment post of an assistant commander for
16     intelligence and security was not filled.

18     could be deployed in offensive and defensive combat actions.  The units
19     included a sabotage unit, tank, logistics, transport, and mortar
20     companies as well as motorised artillery rocket anti-aircraft defence and
21     military police battalions.
