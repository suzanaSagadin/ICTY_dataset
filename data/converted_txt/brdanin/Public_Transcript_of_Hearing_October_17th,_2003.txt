 9            Mr. Brdjanin, can you follow in a language that you can
10    understand?

18    with us today.  Thank you.
19            JUDGE AGIUS:  Thank you.  Appearances for the Defence.
20            MR. CUNNINGHAM:  Good morning, Your Honours.  David Cunningham
