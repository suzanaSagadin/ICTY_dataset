 2                          [Open session]
 3                          --- Upon commencing at 9.05 a.m.
 4            JUDGE AGIUS:  Please be seated.
 5                          [The accused entered court]
 6            JUDGE AGIUS:  Good morning, Mr. Brdjanin.  Can you hear me in a
 7    language that you can understand?
 8            THE ACCUSED BRDJANIN: [Interpretation] Good morning, Your
 9    Honours.  I can hear you and understand you.
10            JUDGE AGIUS:  I thank you.  You may sit down.
11            General Talic, same question to you.  Good morning.  Can you hear
12    me in a language that you can understand?
13            THE ACCUSED TALIC: [Interpretation] Good morning, Your Honours.  I
14    can hear you and understand you.
15            JUDGE AGIUS:  Thank you.  You may sit down.
16            Madam Registrar, you may call the case now, please.
17            THE REGISTRAR:  Yes, Your Honour.  This is the case number,
18    IT-99-36-T, the Prosecutor versus Radoslav Brdjanin and Momir Talic.
19            JUDGE AGIUS:  Yes.  Appearances for the Prosecution.
20            MS. KORNER:  Joanna Korner, assisted by Denise Gustin, case
21    manager.  Good morning Your Honours.
22            JUDGE AGIUS:  Good morning to you.
23            Appearances for Mr. Brdjanin.
24            MR. ACKERMAN:  Your Honour, I'm John Ackerman.  I appear with
25    Tania Radosavljevic.  Good morning.
 1            JUDGE AGIUS:  Good morning to you, Mr. Ackerman.
 2            Appearances for General Talic.
 3            MR. DE ROUX: [Interpretation] Good morning, Mr. President.
 4    Xavier de Roux, assisted by Natasha Fauveau-Ivanovic and Fabien Masson for
 5    General Talic.
 6            JUDGE AGIUS:  Good morning.
 7            Yes, Ms. Korner.
 8            MS. KORNER:  Your Honour, I'm going to ask in a moment to go into
 9    private session.  But before doing so, can I ask Your Honours to make as a
10    matter of urgency an order that the motion that Mr. Ackerman filed on
11    Friday which was filed openly be made confidential.
12            JUDGE AGIUS:  Which one are you referring to?
13            MS. KORNER:  The one that was filed I understand -- I hadn't
14    appreciated until after we left court on Friday that it had been filed.
15    It's the one that relates to the humanitarian organisation.  I then want
16    to go into private session to discuss this.
17            MR. ACKERMAN:  I agree with her request, Your Honour.
18            MS. KORNER:  Your Honour, the reason that I have to do this is
19    unfortunately we were unable to reach the duty judge over the weekend as
20    his mobile phone wasn't left with security.  It needs to be urgent so that
21    nothing -- we've contacted the press office, and we've contacted the
22    Outreach, but it needs to be urgently notified to them that this is not to
23    be released.
24            JUDGE AGIUS:  So Mr. Ackerman, there is no objection on your
25    part?
 1            Yes.  The request -- the Prosecution's request motion is being
 2    granted, and necessary steps must be taken immediately to notify the
 3    Registry with this decision.
 4            Shall we go straight into private session now?
 5            MS. KORNER:  Yes, please.
 6            JUDGE AGIUS:  So Madam Registrar, please let's go into private
 7    session.
