24    Indictment.  The Prosecutor of the International Criminal Tribunal for the
25    Former Yugoslavia, pursuant to her authority under Article 18 of the
 1    Statute of the International Criminal Tribunal for the Former Yugoslavia
 2    and Rule 77 of the Rules of Procedure and Evidence of the International
 3    Criminal Tribunal for the Former Yugoslavia charges:  Beqa Beqaj with
 4    contempt of the Tribunal, attempted content of the Tribunal, and
 5    incitement to contempt of the Tribunal.
 6            The accused, Beqa Beqaj, father's name Zecir, was born on 10th of
 7    November, 1952 in Petrove, Kosovo.  He is a relative of Isak Musliu, an
 8    accused in the case of Prosecutor versus Fatmir Limaj, Haradin Bala, and
 9    Isak Musliu, case number IT-03-66.
10            Charges:  Counts 1 to 3, contempt, attempted contempt, and
11    incitement to contempt of the Tribunal.
12            From on or about 17 February 2003 through to on or about 19th of
13    October, 2004, Beqa Beqaj, individually and in concert with others
14    incited, attempted to commit, committed, or otherwise aided and abetted
15    the commission of contempt of the Tribunal.
16            During the time period set forth above, Beqa Beqaj knowingly and
17    wilfully interfered with the administration of justice by threatening,
18    intimidating, offering a bribe to, or otherwise interfering with witnesses
19    or potential witnesses in the case of Prosecutor versus Limaj, Bala, and
20    Musliu, case number IT-03-66.
21            On or about 11th of June, 2004, in Shtime, Kosovo, the son of Beqa
22    Beqaj accosted (redacted), a potential witness, blaming (redacted)for
23    Isak Musliu's detention in The Hague.  After this incident, on the same
24    day, Beqa Beqaj told (redacted)that Isak Musliu had previously
25    communicated from The Hague that Beqa Beqaj should go to (redacted)and
 1    demand him to withdraw his statement against Isak Musliu.
 2            In early September 2004, Beqa Beqaj contacted (redacted), a
 3  potential witness living outside of Kosovo, through a relative of (redacted)
4  (redacted).  The relative informed (redacted)that Beqa Beqaj had received
 5    calls directly from Isak Musliu in The Hague, and that Isak Musliu told
 6    Beqa Beqaj to tell (redacted)that he must withdraw his statement to the
 7    Tribunal.  It was also suggested that Isak Musliu would reward (redacted)
8  (redacted)with a gift of lands with (redacted)withdrew his statement.
 9            On or shortly before 27 September 2004, Beqa Beqaj again asked the
10    relative to contact (redacted).  The relative told (redacted)that
11    Beqa Beqaj claimed to speak on behalf of Fatmir Limaj and Isak Musliu that
12  (redacted)must travel back to Kosovo to meet with Defence lawyers and
13    withdraw his statement.
14            On or about 6 October 2004, Beqa Beqaj asked (redacted)by
15    telephone to return to Kosovo meet with Fatmir Limaj's brother, Demir
16    Limaj and a lawyer, in order to withdraw his statement against Isak Musliu
17    and Fatmir Limaj and state that he had nothing to do with Musliu and
18    Limaj.  Beqa Beqaj told (redacted)that he had spoken with and acted on
19    behalf of Isak Musliu, Fatmir Limaj, and Demir Limaj.
20            On or about 13 October 2004, Beqa Beqaj again asked (redacted)
21    by telephone to return to Kosovo meet with Demir Limaj or a lawyer in
22    order to withdraw his statement against Isak Musliu and Fatmir Limaj and
23    state that he knows nothing about Musliu and Limaj.
24            By his acts and commissions, and omissions, Beqa Beqaj
25    participated in:
