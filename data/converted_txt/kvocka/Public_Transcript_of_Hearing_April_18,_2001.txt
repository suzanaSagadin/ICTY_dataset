16       Q.   What was their conduct to you when you were in prison and what

19    very sorry that I had to live through all of that and experience it all,
20    and the conditions that myself as a human being had to contend with there,
21    although I was a soldier, were barely bearable.  As a member of the
22    Republika Srpska army, I was taken prisoner, but the torture that I lived
23    through there is a very difficult experience, and I'm sorry to have
24    experienced it as a human being, to have to go through all of them.  They
25    were terrible problems and I can't even begin to explain them.
 1       Q.   In the prison, did you make any statements and, if so, under what
 2    conditions did you make those statements?

 7    statement.

 9       A.   For the most part, the statements that I signed I did not read.
10       Q.   The facts that you presented in those statements, did you ever
11    tell anybody in the prison about them ever?
12       A.   No, never.
