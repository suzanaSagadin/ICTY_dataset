18            The Prosecutor of the International Criminal Tribunal for the
19    former Yugoslavia, pursuant to her authority under Article 18 of the
20    Statute of the International Criminal Tribunal for the former Yugoslavia
21    and Rule 77 of the Rules of Procedure and Evidence of the International
22    Criminal Tribunal for the former Yugoslavia, charges:
23            Avni Krasniqi, with contempt of the Tribunal.
24            The accused, Avni Krasniqi, was born on 18th of May, 1969 in
25    Vranoc in the municipality of Pec in Kosovo.
 1            Charges.  Contempt of the Tribunal.
 2            1.  On 29th of October, 2007, Avni Krasniqi committed - that is
 3    the charge - committed contempt of the Tribunal by knowingly and wilfully
 4    interfering with the administration of justice by failing to comply with
 5    an order to appear before the Trial Chamber in the case of Prosecutor
 6    versus Ramush Haradinaj, Idriz Balaj, and Lahi Brahimaj, Case No.
 7    IT-04-84- this was the correction - T, without just excuse.
 8            2.  On 19th of October, 2007, the Trial Chamber issued a subpoena
 9    ad testificandum against Avni Krasniqi and ordered him to appear to
10    testify before the Tribunal on 29 October 2007.
11            3.  On 23 October 2007, a Prosecution investigator, Ljobomir
12    Jozefciak informed Avni Krasniqi by telephone that the subpoena had been
13    issued against him.  On 24 October 2007, Mr. Jozefciak went to Avni
14    Krasniqi's home and gave him a copy of the subpoena.  Avni Krasniqi
15    refused to sign the receipt of the subpoena but gave Mr. Jozefciak a
16    handwritten explanation for his unwillingness to testify.  Mr. Jozefciak
17    explained to the witness the availability of protective measures which can
18    alleviate Avni Krasniqi's concerns.  Avni Krasniqi insisted that he was
19    not willing to testify.
20            4.  On 25th of October, 2007, UNMIK officer Cardil Kadralj [phoen]
21    served the subpoena on Avni Krasniqi, who received it but refused to sign
22    the memorandum of service and repeated that he was not willing to testify.
23            5.  On 29th of October, 2007, Avni Krasniqi failed to appear
24    before the Trial Chamber without just excuse.
25            By these acts and omissions, Avni Krasniqi committed Contempt of
 1    the Tribunal, punishable under the Tribunal's inherent powers under Rule
 2    77(A)(iii) of the Rules of the Tribunal.
 3            Carla Del Ponte, Prosecutor, The Hague, Netherlands.
