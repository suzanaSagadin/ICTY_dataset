 2            MS. ISSA:  Well, as Your Honour may know, we are still pretty much
 3    in the early stages of this case.  This is I think the only the third

 8    may raise in relation to the ongoing material in this case.
 9            JUDGE LIU:  Thank you.
10            Ms. Jelena Nikolic, do you have anything to mention at this time
11    on this particular issue?
12            MS. NIKOLIC: [Interpretation]  Yes, thank you, Your Honour.  The
13    problem with electronic disclosure or -- the electronic system of
14    disclosure is quite suitable for data searches and this is something that
15    is accessible to the Defence; however, the Defence would like to have all
16    the documents that are relevant, factually and legally, to Mr. Nikolic.
17    We would like to transfer that to our database so that we can create a
18    CaseMap database.  When all the documents are transferred to the database,
19    it's not possible to conduct searches of the material any more.  It means
20    that the analysis of these documents is being made more difficult, unless
21    I am directly connected to the IDS, or the -- the EDS, the electronic
22    database search.  So because of this, our -- we need more time to be able
23    to work and to prepare the case.  For example, a couple of days ago, the
24    transcript from two other trials related to Srebrenica were disclosed to
25    the Defence, the Krstic and Blagojevic Jokic cases.  This is about 26.000
 1    pages of transcripts.  In order to analyse them properly and quickly, it's
 2    possible to do this only electronically.  However, when this is
 3    transferred to the computer and when we start to work in our usual way,
 4    it's not possible to conduct searches and make our own database, which
 5    would be the foundation of creating a CaseMap database.  We have similar
 6    problems regarding the translation of transcripts from other cases.  The
 7    Prosecution is not in a position to translate them and they have sent us,
 8    at least those parts which are part of the supporting material in the
 9    audio version.  They have sent us audios on DVDs so the Defence then only
10    has the option of listening to this material, listening to the DVDs from
11    other cases.
12            And which are -- these transcripts are integral parts of the
13    material that's being disclosed.
14            I think that this would be what I have to say in brief,
15    Your Honour.  Thank you.
16            JUDGE LIU:  Well, as a matter of fact, I'm a layman in the
17    computer science.  I know next to nothing about how to find a document,
18    without the help of other technical assistants.  However, Ms. Jelena, you
19    only presented me a problem.  You did not ask for any relief on this
20    aspect.  And Ms. Issa, would you please be some help to this issue?
21            MS. ISSA:  I'll certainly try, Your Honour.  I believe
22    Mrs. Nikolic actually raised this issue this morning at the 65 ter
23    conference and we advised her that we would look into it, at least
24    preliminarily today, to see if we could find a solution to the problem
25    that she has presented to us and essentially what we've -- my preliminary
 1    answer is we are still presently trying to find a solution to assist
 2    counsel.  But just by way of an explanation, the electronic disclosure
 3    suite system is designed so that a search can only be done within the
 4    actual system itself.  The programme that can highlight a particular word
 5    or name is only associated with the system itself so if one were to try
 6    and pull a document from the EDS system to another folder, that programme
 7    is obviously not -- no longer associated -- would not be associated with
 8    this other folder, and so she cannot do a second search.  But she can
 9    certainly do a search on the EDS system itself.
10            Now, one potential solution we are looking at which we still
11    haven't had an opportunity to confirm is to potentially use the basket
12    that is contained within the system, it is sort of like a folder that's
13    within the EDS system, and it's called a basket.  And what counsel can do
14    is pull documents from within the larger EDS system and put it into the
15    basket to save as their own file and then re-search within the basket
16    itself.  One can do a second search in that basket itself.  That is one
17    solution that we are potentially proposing.  But having said that, we are
18    still looking into the matter and we will try and find a solution as soon
19    as possible.  Having said all of that, of course, the -- you know, the old
20    way was just to provide paper documents to counsel and then this is a
21    far -- certainly an improvement on that in my respectful submission, and
22    it doesn't preclude counsel from doing her searches within the system
23    itself although if we can enhance their ability in some way, we will
24    certainly attempt to do so.

 1    That so-called basket is called "Srebrenica basket" or "Nikolic basket" or
 2    whatever?
 3            MS. ISSA:  Well, at this stage, it's just called an individual
 4    basket is the reference to it.  It might be there might be a potential way
 5    of individualising it to -- for the different counsel.  I'm not entirely
 6    sure about this at this stage because, again, this is simply a preliminary
 7    response that I was able to gather from -- through the computer support
 8    personnel.  So there might be potentially a way of individualising it so
 9    that that basket or folder or whatever one might call it would be
10    individualized to a particular counsel.

13    did?
14            MS. ISSA:  Well, I should just first of all mention that the -- I
15    don't believe the Prosecution actually even is able to pull documents and
16    search them outside of the system so we don't even have that capability.
17    But whether or not we can -- software can be installed to perform that.
18    To be honest at this stage, Your Honour, I'm simply not able to respond to
19    that question but that's something that I'm perfectly happy to look into
20    and determine and would certainly advise counsel.
