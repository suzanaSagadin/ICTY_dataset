 1                          Wednesday, 20 April 2005
 2                          [Open session]
 3                          [Further initial appearance]
 4                          [The accused entered court]
 5                          --- Upon commencing at 2.30 p.m.
 6            JUDGE LIU:  Good afternoon, ladies and gentlemen.  Could Mr. Court
 7    Deputy call the case, please?
 8            THE REGISTRAR: Good afternoon, Your Honours.  This is case number
 9    IT-02-63-I, the Prosecutor versus Drago Nikolic.
10            JUDGE LIU:  Thank you very much.  May I have the appearances,
11    please?  For the Prosecution?
12            MR. McCLOSKEY:  Good afternoon, Your Honour, Peter McCloskey and
13    with me today is Janet Stewart, as always.
14            JUDGE LIU:  Thank you.
15            For Defence?
16            MR. VAN DER SPOEL:  Good afternoon, Your Honour.  Tjarda van der
17    Spoel, Duty Counsel for Mr. Nikolic.
18            JUDGE LIU:  Thank you.
19            Well, we'll continue the initial appearance of the accused,
20    Mr. Drago Nikolic, pursuant to Rule 62 of the Rules of Procedure and
21    Evidence.
22            Mr. Spoel, could I ask you a question, please?
23            MR. VAN DER SPOEL:  Of course, you may, Your Honour.
24            JUDGE LIU:  Are you the Duty Judge or the so-called permanent
25    Judge for Mr. Nikolic.
 1            MR. VAN DER SPOEL:  I'm the duty counsel, Your Honour.
 2            JUDGE LIU:  I'm sorry, I've been sitting here for the whole day.
 3    Do you have an opportunity to discuss the indictment with your client?
 4            MR. VAN DER SPOEL:  Yes, I did yesterday afternoon, Your Honour.
 5            JUDGE LIU:  I see.  Do you think your client is ready to plead
 6    today?
 7            MR. VAN DER SPOEL:  I know my client is ready to plead today,
 8    Your Honour.
 9            JUDGE LIU:  Let me ask Mr. Nikolic.  Mr. Nikolic, can you follow
10    the proceedings in a language that you understand?
11            THE ACCUSED: [Interpretation] Yes, I can, Your Honour.
12            JUDGE LIU:  Are you ready to plead today?
13            THE ACCUSED: [Interpretation] Yes.
14            JUDGE LIU:  Well, you have the right to have the whole indictment
15    read out or you may waive this right.  Which way do you prefer?
16            THE ACCUSED: [Interpretation] Your Honour, there is no need to
17    read out the entire indictment.  I have read the indictment and I fully
18    understand the indictment.
19            JUDGE LIU:  Thank you very much.
20            So I will now read the individual charges brought against you, and
21    I will then ask you whether you plead guilty or not guilty to the specific
22    charge.  Your answer in each case may be guilty or not guilty as the case
23    may be.  You understand that?
24            THE ACCUSED: [Interpretation] I do, Your Honour.
25            JUDGE LIU:  Thank you.  Let's begin.
 1            Count 1 charges you with A, genocide, punishable under Article
 2    4(3) and 7(1) of the Statute of the Tribunal or alternatively, B,
 3    complicity in genocide, punishable under Article 4(3)(E) and 7(1) of the
 4    Statute of the Tribunal.  How do you plead, guilty or not guilty?
 5            THE ACCUSED: [Interpretation] Not guilty.
 6            JUDGE LIU:  Very well.
 7            Count 2 charges you with extermination as a crime against humanity
 8    punishable under Article 5(B) and 7(1) of the Statute of the Tribunal.  Do
 9    you plead guilty or not guilty?
10            THE ACCUSED: [Interpretation] Not guilty, Your Honour.
11            JUDGE LIU:  Count 3 charges you with murder as a crime against
12    humanity punishable under Article 5(A) and 7(1) of the Statute of the
13    Tribunal.  How do you plead, guilty or not guilty?
14            THE ACCUSED: [Interpretation] Not guilty.
15            JUDGE LIU:  Count 4 charges you with murder as a violation of the
16    laws and the customs of war, punishable under Article 3 and 7(1) of the
17    Statute of the Tribunal.  Do you plead guilty or not guilty?
18            THE ACCUSED: [Interpretation] Not guilty.
19            JUDGE LIU:  Count 5 charges you with persecutions on political,
20    racial and religious grounds as a crime against humanity, including
21    murder, cruel and inhumane treatment, terrorising the civilian population
22    and the destruction of personal property, punishable under Articles 5(H)
23    and 7(1) of the Statute of the Tribunal.  How do you plead, guilty or not
24    guilty?
25            THE ACCUSED: [Interpretation] Not guilty.

 1            JUDGE LIU:  Thank you, sir.  You may sit down, please.
 2            May the Registrar please take the note of the plea to the counts
 3    of the indictment by Mr. Nikolic.  The Registrar will be instructed to fix
 4    a date for a trial when appropriate.  The accused is to remain in custody
 5    until further order.
 6            At this stage, I would like to remind the Prosecution that,
 7    pursuant to Rule 66 of the Rules of Procedure and Evidence, it has 30 days
 8    to disclose all the supporting materials, with the indictment.
 9            At the same time, the Defence is also advised that it will have 30
10    days' period for filing any preliminary motions once it has received all
11    the supporting materials in accordance with Rule 66 of the Rules.
12            For the expeditious preparation of the case, a Status Conference
13    will be scheduled within 120 days from today, pursuant to Rule 65 bis (A).
14      At this stage, are there any other matters that the Prosecution wishes
15    to raise?  Mr. McCloskey?
16            MR. McCLOSKEY:  Yes, Your Honour.  We have the supporting material
17    and it's my understanding that the Registry or OLAD is -- and Mr. Nikolic
18    almost have a permanent lawyer and hopefully in the next few days he will
19    have one.  And it's my understanding that he would prefer that the
20    permanent lawyer had the -- actually get the material so we don't put duty
21    counsel at having to deal with that responsibility.
22            Also, just to inform the Court and Mr. Nikolic, it is the
23    Prosecution's intention to offer a motion to join all the Srebrenica
24    accused that are now in custody, and at least one that is not in custody,
25    and we are in the process of drafting that motion and hope to have it done
 1    in the next -- next couple of weeks.
 2            JUDGE LIU:  Thank you very much for your information.
 3            Mr. Spoel?
 4            MR. VAN DER SPOEL:  Thank you, Your Honour.  It is correct that
 5    Mr. Nikolic is in the process of obtaining assigned counsel so I think it
 6    would be wise that the supporting material would be given to that counsel.
 7            JUDGE LIU:  Thank you very much.  But how soon would you please
 8    let me know that Mr. Nikolic will have a permanent lawyer?
 9            MR. VAN DER SPOEL:  I understand from the Registry and from
10    Mr. Nikolic it will be a question of about a week or two, as soon as
11    possible, I understand.  I cannot pinpoint it to a day.
12            JUDGE LIU:  I see.  Well, it is my hope that Mr. Nikolic could
13    have a permanent lawyer as early as possible so that he could discuss the
14    case with that lawyer and prepare his case.
15            Are there any matters that you would like to raise at there stage,
16    Mr. Spoel?
17            MR. VAN DER SPOEL:  No, thank you, Your Honour.
18            JUDGE LIU:  Thank you.
19            Mr. Nikolic, do you have anything to complain during the past
20    month while you were in the detention unit?
21            THE ACCUSED: [Interpretation] No, no complaints, Your Honour.
22            JUDGE LIU:  How about your health?
23            THE ACCUSED: [Interpretation] My health is excellent.
24            JUDGE LIU:  Thank you very much.  I believe that you have to take
25    care of yourself, because that case might be a very long case.
 1            You may sit down.
 2            Well, I believe that's all for this initial appearance, and the
 3    hearing is adjourned.
 4                          --- Whereupon the Further Initial Appearance
 5                     adjourned at 2.40 p.m.
