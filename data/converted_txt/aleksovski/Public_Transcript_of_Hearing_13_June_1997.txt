     3      THE CLERK OF THE COURT:   Case number IT-95-14-PT, the
     4          Prosecutor versus Tihomir Blaskic.
     5      JUDGE McDONALD:   Could I have the appearances please?
     6      MR HARMON:   Good afternoon, Madame President, Judge Odio
     7          Benito, Judge Jan. My name is Mark Harmon.   I am
     8          representing the Prosecutor's Office today.
     9      JUDGE McDONALD:   Fine.  Thank you, Mr Harmon.
    10      MME VIDOVIC:   Good afternoon, your Honours.   I am Vasvir
    11          Vidovic, Minister Counsellor of the Embassy of
    12          Bosnia-Herzegovina in The Hague and the BH
    13          representative with the Kingdom of the Netherlands.
    14      JUDGE McDONALD:   Fine.   Thank you.   On May 29th, 1997 the
    15          Trial Chamber entered an order to give evidence.  It was
    16          directed to Mr Mato Tadic, Minister of Justice for the
    17          Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina, or his
    18          representative from the Ministry of Justice to appear
    19          before the Chamber on Friday, 13th June 1997 at 15:00
    20          hours to show cause why the subpoena duces tecum of 30th
    21          April 1997 had not been complied with and to be prepared
    22          to inform the Trial Chamber what affirmative steps were
    23          taken to serve the subpoena duces tecum on Mr Stjepan
    24          Ivankovic.
    25                Mr Harmon, do you have any matters you wish to
     1          present regard this matter?
     2      MR HARMON:   Your Honour, I have two matters I would like to
     3          raise initially with the Trial Chamber.   The first is
     4          to report that since the last court order again there
     5          has been no production of any documents whatsoever to
     6          the Prosecutor's office in response to the subpoena that
     7          you issued.
     8                My second request is that we go into closed
     9          session, because I would like to report some matters to
    10          the Trial Chamber in confidence.
    11      JUDGE McDONALD:   Miss Vidovic, do you have any objection to
    12          the Trial Chamber proceeding in closed session?
    13      MME VIDOVIC:   No, your Honour.   I'm in agreement with
    14          this.
    15      JUDGE McDONALD:   Thank you.   Well, we will go into closed
    16          session.   Before, just to put this matter into some
    17          framework, for several months now, I suppose, we have
    18          had several hearings regarding subpoenas duces tecum.
    19          Bosnia-Herzegovina has not opposed the power or not
    20          contended that the Trial Chamber lacks the power to
    21          issue a subpoena duces tecum.   Instead, we have been
    22          attempting to have the various subpoenas served and have
    23          the documents requested provided to the Prosecutor.
    24          Thus far we have been unsuccessful, but we will continue
    25          in closed session and see what steps have been taken, as
     1          well as how we should decide how we should proceed from
     2          here.   So we will stand in recess, I believe, for five
     3          minutes and then go on to closed session.
