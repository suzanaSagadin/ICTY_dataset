18            JUDGE MOLOTO:  Thank you very much.  We've got a couple of minutes
19    before we are due to break.  Just want to deal with this one important
20    housekeeping matter.
21            This very witness who has just stood down once again has gone far
22    beyond the time allocated to him, and at this rate I don't see how we are
23    going to keep within the time-limits.  And I just want to say to the
24    parties that the Chamber wants to warn you now that it is going to adhere
25    very strictly to the time-limits.
 1            The Chamber is mindful of the fact that the Defence has submitted
 2    further 65 ter estimates, and in the light of that, the Chamber is going
 3    to give you a revised 73 ter decision regulating the number of hours and
 4    we are going to have to deal -- stick to those hours.
 5            Mr. Milovancevic, earlier you said you didn't understand what I
 6    meant by one-third time.  What I wanted to say about that time was, having
 7    realised that trying to divide the time on a 50/50 basis between the
 8    Defence and the Prosecution, this obviously leaves us no time for
 9    questions by the Bench and questions for re-examination by the Defence.
10    The Bench is now going to insist that when a third of your time is over
11    you stop leading, and the Prosecution must try to cross-examine within the
12    next third, and the last third of the time is going to be used for
13    re-examination and questions by the Bench.  That's how we are going to
14    regulate the time.  You're going to have to, both of you, all of us, are
15    going to have to learn to economise with time and put very succinct
16    questions to the witness and control the witness to give us succinct and
17    pertinent answers without any frills attached to the answer.
18            So that's how we are going to do it.  As soon as the 73 ter
19    decision is out, we are going to go by that estimation.  For the time
20    being, we shall go by the estimation that was given by the Defence
21    before -- the latest 65 ter estimation.
22            Yes, Mr. Milovancevic.
