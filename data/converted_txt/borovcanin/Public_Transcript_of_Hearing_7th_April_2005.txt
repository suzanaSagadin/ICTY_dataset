10    you were ordered to report to Radislav Krstic, who was then Chief of Staff
11    of the Drina Corps of the VRS, which is the Bosnian Serb army.  The
12    indictment also alleges that units under your command were deployed in and
13    around the areas of Potocari, Sandici, Kravica, and Zvornik from the 12th
14    of July to the 18th of July of 1995.
15            In the several days following the attack on Srebrenica, according
16    to the Prosecutor, VRS and MUP forces captured, detained, summarily
17    executed, and buried over 7.000 Bosnian Muslim men and boys from the
18    Srebrenica enclave, and in addition forcibly transferred the Bosnian
19    Muslim women and children of Srebrenica out of the enclave.  The
20    indictment which was confirmed against you, Mr. Borovcanin, refers to your
21    alleged involvement in the opportunistic killings in Potocari,
22    opportunistic killings in Bratunac, wide-scale and organised killings in
23    Potocari and Tisca, killings and mistreatment of prisoners captured along
24    the Bratunac-Milici road, and wide-scale and organised killings in the
25    Zvornik area as well as other opportunistic killings.
