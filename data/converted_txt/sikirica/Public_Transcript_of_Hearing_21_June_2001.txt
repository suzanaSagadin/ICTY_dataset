 4            Firstly, the Chamber reaffirms the test set out in Kordic and
 5    Cerkez for the application of Rule 98 bis, that is, whether there is
 6    evidence on the basis of which a reasonable Tribunal could convict.  It
 7    does not understand the reference in Kunarac to reasonable doubt to imply
 8    that the Trial Chamber must be satisfied beyond a reasonable doubt of the
 9    guilt of the accused in a particular case before it can refuse a
10    submission under Rule 98 bis.  Rather, the reference in Kunarac is a gloss
11    on the meaning of "could convict" and there is no contradiction between
12    the two decisions.
13            On that basis, the Chamber makes the following rulings:
