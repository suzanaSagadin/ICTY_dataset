 4             JUDGE MERON:  Thank you, Mr. Lukic.
 5             As you are aware, Rule 65 ter (B) of the Rules of Procedure and
 6     Evidence requires a Status Conference to be convened within 120 days
 7     after the filing of a Notice of Appeal, and, thereafter, within 120 days
 8     after the last Status Conference for each Appellant in the Tribunal's
 9     custody.
10             A Status Conference serves two primary purposes.  First, it
11     allows the Appellants an opportunity to express concerns relating to
12     their appeal or detention conditions.  Second, it provides an opportunity
13     to update the Appellants with respect to the status of their case.  The
14     Status Conference is also an opportunity for the Tribunal to inquire
15     about the mental and physical condition of the detained persons.
16             In the present case, the last Status Conference was held on
17     7 March 2012.  Today's Status Conference was scheduled by an order --
18     order issued on 18 June 2012.
19             Let me therefore start the substantive part of this
20     Status Conference by inquiring into Mr. Perisic's health.
21             Mr. Perisic, if you prefer this discussion to be held in closed
22     session, please indicate and I will ...
23             THE APPELLANT: [Interpretation] Yes.  I would like to speak in
24     closed session, please.
25             JUDGE MERON:  Registrar, please close the session.
