11     career when I graduated from the military academy with the rank of second
12     lieutenant, and then I was promoted in service, and in 1993 when I had my
13     last duty at the Ministry of Defence I was a colonel, but I was in the
14     position of a general.
15        Q.   Could you explain that to us, please, how one could have the rank
16     of a colonel and be in the position of a general.
17        A.   It is possible, because this is how it is regulated in the
18     system.  In order to be awarded the rank of a general, a colonel has
19     previously to be appointed to such a post for one year, and if during
20     that one year it turns out that it was justified to assign him to such a
21     position, he's then awarded the general's rank.  It's the usual systemic
22     solution because one cannot be awarded the rank of a general without a
23     formation post existing previously, and the person has to spend at least
24     one year on such a post so that his abilities would be proved and it
25     could be seen if he can be awarded a general's rank or not.
