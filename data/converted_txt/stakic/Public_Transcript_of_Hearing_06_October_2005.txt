20    actually reached by the Trial Chamber.  The fact that a different
21    reasonable Trial Chamber, or even this Appeals Chamber sitting as an
22    original trier of fact, might have come to a different conclusion on the
23    same evidence is not sufficient to meet the standard of review on appeal.

24    omission will cause death."

22    that crime.  Therefore, once the awareness of a substantial likelihood is
23    proven, the additional volitional element is proven as well.
