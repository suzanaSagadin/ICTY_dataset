 1             THE APPELLANT:  [In English] May I sit?
 2             JUDGE MERON:  Yes, please.
 3             This is a Status Conference called in accordance with Rule 65 bis
 4     of the Rules of Procedure and Evidence.  Rule 65 bis (B) requires that a
 5     Status Conference be convened within 120 days of the last Status
 6     Conference to allow any person in custody pending appeal the opportunity
 7     to raise issues in relation to thereto, including the mental and physical
 8     condition of the person.
 9             Our last Status Conference in this case was on September 2, 2004.
10             I would like now to ask Mr. Stakic whether you have any issues to
11     raise in relation to your detention and whether you would like to express
12     any concerns regarding your mental or physical well-being.
13             Mr. Stakic.
14             THE APPELLANT: [Interpretation] Your Honour, I must say that
15     since late November or early December I have had high blood pressure.
16     This did happen earlier, but it was always somewhere about 150/90 with
17     occasional headaches, which I paid no heed to.  Being a doctor myself, I
18     thought that was on account of the climate or the weather changing or
19     difficulty with my sinuses.  However, now the pressure was 165/115.  I
20     thought my head was about to burst.  Eventually I got a prescription from
21     the doctor.  I was taking medicines for a while.  It would go down and
22     then rise to 160/60 after a couple of days.  I have received some lab
23     results in the meantime which seem to be fine.
24             I have been diagnosed with a post-stress reaction.  I'm receiving
25     therapy three times a day over a time period of six months; we shall be
 1     monitoring the results and see what happens.
 2             Occasionally I've had the same high pressure, 150/90, 150/95, but
 3     I don't think that this should cause any further problems.  As for the
 4     medical staff and other staff at the Detention Unit, I have no complaints
 5     whatsoever.  They have been most helpful and I have received all the
 6     assistance required.  My lab results have been processed and treatment
 7     has been administered.
 8             JUDGE MERON:  Thank you, Mr. Stakic.  I am pleased to hear that
 9     despite the problems that you had in the context of your blood pressure,
10     that you have received good medical treatment which is continuing.  And I
11     do hope that the problem will get better and better.
12             I take it that there is nothing else that you would like to tell
13     me today?  In which case, you may sit down.
14             THE APPELLANT: [Interpretation] I have been on therapy.  I would
15     just like to say that we're treating the symptoms here, not the cause,
16     unfortunately.  Other than that, I have nothing to say.  Thank you.
17     Thank you for your kind words, Your Honour.
18             JUDGE MERON:  Thank you very much, Mr. Stakic.  Do sit down.

23     can.
