 8    had to wait and I have had to wait also.
 9            So this hearing is now starting today, and I wish to recall that
10    according to the Rules, the Pre-Trial Judge shall convene a Status
11    Conference within 120 days of the initial appearance of the accused.  And
12    since the last time was the 22nd of June, we have to meet again.  So I
13    will examine the motions which may be pending and also see about the

15            As you know, after a motion from the Prosecution the Referral
16    Bench by a decision of 8 July 2005 referred the case to the authorities of
17    Bosnia-Herzegovina, the judicial authorities, by application applying
18    Article 11 bis of the Rules.  But the Defence of the accused Todovic, as
19    well as the Prosecution, appealed from this decision on the 8th of July,
20    2005.  And therefore, we are waiting on all sides the decision of the
21    Chamber of appeal after the appeals which have been filed.
22            For the other motions which are pending there is still one which
23    is on stand-by.  It's a preliminary motion of the Defence of Todovic,
24    accused, which was filed on the 10th of March, 2005, applying Article
25    72(A)(ii) of the Rules.  I will recall in my memory that on the 23rd of
 1    March, 2005, the Chamber had accepted the motion -- had granted the -- it
 2    was to have a stay of a decision on the motion of the accused Todovic on
 3    the 10th of March, 2005, awaiting a motion from the Prosecution to be
 4    allowed to modify -- to amend the indictment.  And then on the 17th of
 5    May, 2005, the Chamber took an order asking the Prosecutor to establish a
 6    new indictment.  On 25th of May, 2005, the Prosecution filed a motion
 7    asking the Chamber leave to amend the indictments.  The Defence of the
 8    accused Todovic as well as the Defence for Todovic [as interpreted] filed
 9    their submissions following Article 72 of the Rules.
10            On the 4th of July and 10th July, 2005, the Prosecution and the
11    Defence of the accused filed respectively their response -- their replies.
12    The Judges of the Chamber, whom I consulted, considered that one should
13    await the decision on the question of the referral of the case to the
14    judicial authorities of Bosnia-Herzegovina, because if this case were
15    finally referred to the authorities of Bosnia-Herzegovina, it will be for
16    the Prosecutor -- the local Prosecutor to draft an indictment since the
17    referral has been ordered from a case file concerning facts.  And the
18    legal qualification of facts will be then done by the local prosecutor.
19    Therefore, it appears to me difficult for the Judges of the Chamber to
20    pronounce on legal issues when the local prosecutor has still to decide on
21    these things according to his own indictment.  This is the reason why we
22    decided to await the decision of the Referral Bench.  Since the Referral
23    Bench has now to await a appeal we have to wait serenely the decision of
24    the Appeals Chamber.
25            As from that there will be two possibilities.  One, if the Chamber
 1    of appeal confirms -- affirms the decision of the 8th of July, 2005, this
 2    will mean that this case is indeed referred to the Bosnia-Herzegovinian
 3    authorities.  And therefore, the Chamber to which I belong will make a
 4    decision, citing that he will not have to rule on the matters of law since
 5    it will be the local bench we shall have -- the local prosecutor who will
 6    have to draft the indictment.
 7            On the other hand, if the Appeals Chamber decided that the case
 8    will stay with ICTY, henceforth in the next following days -- in the ten
 9    next following days we will render our own decision on the motion which
10    is presently pending.  And therefore, there will be no prejudice for any
11    of the accused inasmuch as we will be in a position to render our
12    decision.
13            So I wanted to inform the parties of this question, this issue,
14    because I consider that it's not necessary if the case is referred to
15    another court, I don't think we should have to decide about the legal
16    definitions when -- in a case in which the local prosecutor may have to
17    draft his own indictment pursuant to Bosnia-Herzegovina law.  Therefore,
18    this being said, I will now speak about the disclosure.  You got Article
19    66(A)(i) and (A)(ii) and Article 68 of the Rules, Rule 68.  And I will
20    give the floor to here from each side what are the requests or comments.
21            First of all, I am going to ask the Prosecution whether the
22    disclosures according to Rule 66(A)(i) have been done and are continuing
23    or not.
