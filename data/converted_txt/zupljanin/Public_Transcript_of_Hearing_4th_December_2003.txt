16            The Trial Chamber shall ensure that a trial is fair and

18    rules of procedure and evidence, with full respect for the rights of the
19    accused and due regard for the protection of victims and witnesses.
20            A person against whom an indictment has been confirmed shall,
21    pursuant to an order or an arrest warrant of the International Tribunal,
22    be taken into custody, immediately informed of the charges against him,
23    and transferred to the International Tribunal.
24            The Trial Chamber shall read the indictment, satisfy itself that
25    the rights of the accused are respected, confirm that the accused
 1    understands the indictment and instruct the accused to enter a plea.  The
 2    Trial Chamber shall set a day for trial.
 3            The hearings should be public unless the Trial Chamber decides to
 4    close the proceedings in accordance with its rules of procedure and
 5    evidence.
 6            Article 21, rights of the accused.
 7            All persons shall be equal before the International Tribunal.
 8            In the determination of charges against him, the accused shall be
 9    entitled to a fair and public hearing subject to Article 22 of the
10    Statute.
11            The accused shall be presumed innocent until proved guilty
12    according to the provisions of the present Statute.
13            In the determination of any charge against the accused pursuant to
14    the present Statute, the accused shall be entitled to the following
15    minimum guarantees in full equality:
16            (A)  to be informed properly and in detail in a language which he
17    understands of the nature and cause of the charge against him;
18            (B)  to have adequate time and facilities for the preparation of
19    his defence and to communicate with counsel of his own choosing;
20            (C)  to be tried without undue delay;
21            (D)  to be tried in his presence, and to defend himself in person
22    or through legal assistance of his own choosing; to be informed, if he
23    does not have legal assistance, of this right; and to have legal
24    assistance assigned to him in any case where the interests of justice so
25    require, and without payment by him in any such case if he does not have
 1    sufficient means to pay for it;
 2            (E)  to examine, or have examined, the witnesses against him and
 3    to obtain the attendance and examinations of witnesses on his behalf under
 4    the same conditions as witnesses against him;
 5            (F)  to have the free assistance of an interpreter if he cannot
 6    understand or speak the language used in the International Tribunal.
 7            (G)  not to be compelled to testify against himself or to confess
