17     statement and seeing how many incidents this witness covered in his

24     scheduling.

 1     to resolve the problem with the Prosecution as far as scheduling is
 2     concerned.  As far as the timing is concerned, the Chamber might re-visit
 3     the way of cross-examination soon.  We are still thinking about these
 4     matters also time-wise.
 5             Having said this, is there any other matter?  Not.
 6             Then we hear the -- I would like -- first of all, I would like to
 7     put on the record publicly that by decision of the 15th of January of
 8     this year it -- the Chamber has granted the Prosecution's request for the
 9     augmentation of protective measures for witness RM009.  And on the 17th
10     of January, the Chamber has granted the Prosecution's request to add
11     three documents to the Prosecution's 65 ter list to be used with
