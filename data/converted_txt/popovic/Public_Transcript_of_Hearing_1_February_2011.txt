13             The witness was a member of the Zvornik Brigade's military police
14     company based at the brigade command in the Standard factory in Karakaj.
15     In July 1995, the witness served as the driver for the brigade's chief of
16     security, Drago Nikolic.
17             In the morning of 14 July 1995, the brigade's deputy chief of
18     security, Milorad Trbic, ordered the witness to pick up Drago Nikolic at
19     the forward command post at Kitovnica and bring Nikolic back to the
20     brigade command for a meeting with Main Staff security chief Ljubisa
21     Beara and Drina Corps security chief Vujadin Popovic.  The witness saw
22     Beara and Popovic arrive and enter the barracks.  At approximately 0730
23     or 0800 hours, give or take an hour, the witness left Standard in his
24     car, an Opel Rekord, picked up Nikolic at the IKM, and returned to
25     Standard about half an hour later.
 1             Nikolic went to his office for the meeting with Beara and
 2     Popovic.  The witness waited in another room, and when Nikolic came out
 3     of the meeting, about 15 or 20 minutes later, he was somber and angry
 4     because he was not consulted beforehand and was ordered to find
 5     accommodation for the prisoners.  Nikolic told him that there would be
 6     some people coming in for exchange.  Nikolic told the witness that the
 7     witness was supposed to drive Nikolic to the Vidikovac hotel because
 8     Nikolic had been ordered to go there to wait for the buses.
 9             At approximately 0800 or 0900 hours, give or take an hour, they
10     drove to the Vidikovac hotel and about five minutes after arriving a
11     convoy of five or ten buses arrived carrying Muslim men guarded by
12     civilian policemen in blue uniforms from an unknown unit.
13             Nikolic told the witness to board the first bus and go to
14     Orahovac and Nikolic took the car and told the witness that he would do
15     some errands.
16             The bus stopped at the primary school in Orahovac.  The prisoners
17     were taken from the buses into the school gymnasium by the policemen on
18     the buses.  The prisoners left their belongings in a part of the
19     courtyard.  There were no prisoners at the school when he arrived.
20             Zvornik Brigade military police arrived approximately 20 to 30
21     minutes later including Trbic, Cedo Jovic, Goran Bogdanovic, a female MP
22     named Nada, the witness's cousin, Stanoje Bircakovic, and the commander
23     of the military police company, Miomir Jasikovac.  The MPs provided
24     security around the school.  There were also a lot of curious local
25     population there.  About half an hour after the prisoners arrived, the
 1     witness saw Vujadin Popovic at the school with two of Popovic's men.
 2             At approximately 1100 hours, just after the prisoners had been
 3     taken into the gym, Drago Nikolic arrived at the school in the Opel
 4     Rekord, gave the witness the car keys, then spoke to Jasikovac and other
 5     people.  Nikolic stayed for approximately an hour then departed, but left
 6     the Opel at the school.
 7             During this time, the witness was standing in front of the school
 8     which he never entered.
 9             Small TAM trucks then arrived at the school and backed up to the
10     door.  Prisoners with their hands tied were loaded on to the trucks which
11     drove away, each with approximately 20 to 30 prisoners.
12             Later, prisoners boarding the trucks were also blindfolded.
13     After two or three trips, an hour or so, the witness was ordered to the
14     best of his memory by Jasikovac to follow the trucks to a water source
15     then return to the school.
16             The witness did this four or five times following the trucks in
17     the Opel.  From the water source, the trucks continued on.
18             In the evening, just before nightfall, Nikolic returned to the
19     school, stayed for about half an hour to an hour, then told the witness
