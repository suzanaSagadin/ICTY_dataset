 6    Defence successfully, as it turned out, tendered his previous statements.
 7            The Defence was challenged by the Prosecution as to the relevance
 8    of the tendering of those statements, and their explanation was that it
 9    went to his credibility.  Credibility can only arise as an issue if there
10    is something in the witness's evidence which the Defence does not accept.

13    not accepted by the Defence when they did not put to him any aspect of his
