 5    from the Vranica building, which faces the parking lot.  And my building
 6    and the Vranica building had a joint parking lot, parking space.  It was
 7    just 60 or 70 -- they were just 60 or 70 metres apart, so that our windows
 8    faced each other, faced onto the parking lot.  My windows and their
 9    windows faced the parking lot.  And there was firing at the windows.  So
10    not only me and my family and our apartment, but all the tenants of
11    Stjepan Radic Street number 74 spent 36 hours without being able to get up
12    off the floor.  The windows were all shattered, bullet-ridden.
13       Q.   Who did the shooting?  Who shot at you?
14       A.   Well, we couldn't see, but the bullets were coming from the
15    Vranica building.  That was the only place that those bullets could have
16    come from, and that was where the soldiers of the BH army were.
17       Q.   And after 36 hours, 36 hours later?
18       A.   Thirty-six hours later the fighting stopped, subsided, and we went
19    to take a look out of our windows.  The shooting stopped, and when we went
20    up to the windows, we saw that it was no longer the BH army that was there
21    but that they were the HVO soldiers.  And from the last entrance to the
22    Vranica building, we saw people with white flags raised.  There might have
23    been about 20 to 25 of them, citizens.
24            I noticed on the occasion that Zijo Demirovic was in front of
25    them, the former president of the SDA party for the Bosnia-Herzegovina
 1    region.  He was standing in front of them.  And I was confirmed that this
 2    was so by an HVO soldier.  Several HVO soldiers, once they had taken over
 3    the Vranica building, entered our own building.  And he brought in a bag,
 4    a nice-looking bag.  And as he recognised me and knew that I was a member
 5    of the Presidency, he gave me this bag and said, "I have some important
 6    documents here that I found in an apartment belonging to Zijo," or
 7    whoever.  "Do you want to take possession of this bag, Mr. Boras?"  And I
 8    said, "No.  You keep the bag and give it to your superior officer,
 9    commanding officer, and he'll know what to do with it."
10       Q.   You said that citizens came out with the flags.  Were they
11    citizens or were they soldiers?
12       A.   They were all wearing civilian clothing.
13       Q.   I see.  They were all wearing civilian clothing.  And who led
14    them?
15       A.   Well, I was nearby.  You could see the people, and I knew Mr. Zijo
16    himself.  I had met him several times before the fighting.  I met him in
17    the office of the -- of Jovan Topic and Petar Zelenika, together with
18    Orucevic, right before the fighting started, a month before the fighting.
19    They had very close ties.  So I would go to visit Topic from time to
20    time.  I would stop by and would find them there on several occasions.  We
21    even had a cup of coffee together several times and discussed the problems
22    that existed in Mostar and the whole situation there.  And I -- not even
23    in my wildest dreams did I ever think that there would be any fighting.
