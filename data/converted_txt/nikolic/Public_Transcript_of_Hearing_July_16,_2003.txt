21    It was -- there were very intense attacks, and I saw people leaving

14    DutchBat, they were forcing us out.  They were telling us:  "Go to
15    Potocari."  So we had to go to Potocari.  We had no other choice.  All the
16    people who stayed behind in Srebrenica, they were all killed.  They did

 1    soldiers at that time.

 4    about the time.  That day, we arrived in Potocari possibly around noon.

10    couldn't move.  As soon as you moved some way, you'd brush against
11    somebody.  But my brother went and sat next to a haystack, and we just put
12    some hay and sat down by a lorry that was completely destroyed by shells.
13    We spent the night under that lorry on that hay.
14       Q.   All right.  And did you have any food or water with you at the

16       A.   No, we had nothing.  It all happened so quickly and it was such
17    panic that we didn't even think of that.  To be honest, we were not that
18    hungry.  In all that fear, it didn't cross our mind.
19       Q.   Moving then to July 12, the next day, can you tell us what
20    happened the next morning when you woke up?
21       A.   Yes, I can.  We woke up.  We didn't sleep much that night.  It got
22    light, and at about 10.00, there was a lot of panic among the people.
23    Children are crying.  Women are crying.  It was very difficult.  But at
24    about 10.00, we saw, and people started to shout from the village of
25    Pecista, Djogazi, from Budak.  Soldiers started appearing, and they were
 1    surrounding us there in Potocari.  And within a very short while, they
 2    came to us, and they started to walk among the people and to say that they
 3    wouldn't do anything to anyone, that we shouldn't worry, we shouldn't cry,
 4    that we shouldn't panic, that we shouldn't escape, that nothing would --
 5    no harm would come to us, that buses would come about 3.00 or 4.00 at the
 6    latest.  That we would be taken to Tuzla, that we would be taken to the
 7    free territory.  That's what they said.  This isn't yours; you should go
 8    over there.  And they said if this had been done in the beginning of the
 9    war, that none of this would have happened.  They said you could have left
10    the beginning of the war.
