 4                           --- Upon commencing at 4.04 p.m.
 5             JUDGE ROBINSON:  Let me say first that in the absence of
 6     Judge Van Den Wyngaert, Judge David and I sit pursuant to the provisions
 7     of Rule 15 bis.
 8             There are two matters that we could usefully dispose of in the
 9     absence of witnesses.  The first relates to the query raised by the
10     Prosecutor, and this is in connection with the witness who had been
11     subpoenaed but who has refused to come to court, and the question which
12     the Prosecutor raised was whether that witness should forthwith be
13     arrested or whether proceedings would be instituted by way of contempt
14     pursuant to Rule 77.
15             This is the Trial Chamber's ruling on the matter:  The
16     authorities should endeavour once more to secure the attendance of the
17     subpoenaed witness.  Should the witness still refuse to attend, the
18     witness will be liable to contempt proceedings pursuant to Rule 77.
19             I hope that is sufficiently clear.
20             The second matter relates to Prosecution motion of the 8th of
21     September, and I believe also the 9th of September under 92 quater, and
22     it relates to VG-025.  We should be in private session for this, in
23     private session.
