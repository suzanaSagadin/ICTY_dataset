18             I can now provide a summary of this witness's evidence.
19             The witness is from the village of Vladovo in the Gnjilane
20     municipality.  He describes the entry of the VJ and armed civilians into
21     his village area on the 25th of March, 1999, and their activities which
22     included provocations, setting up check-points, and stealing vehicles.
23     That caused much alarm and, as a result, the witness left his village at
24     the end of March 1999 and went to the village of Vlastica.  He stayed in
25     the surrounding mountains with a number of others for two weeks.  And
 1     from his position he could observe with a pair of binoculars VJ soldiers
 2     looting and setting houses on fire in the surrounding area.  He also saw
 3     the village of Vlastica being attacked, looted, and buildings, including
 4     the mosque, being burned by VJ soldiers.
 5             After two weeks in the mountains they ran out of food.  The
 6     witness went to Presevo in Serbia proper and then to the village of Zub.
 7     As the VJ was present in that village, people were fleeing towards the
 8     village of Sefer.  The witness joined these people and they proceeded
 9     towards Presevo, then continuing to walk for about 12 hours.  The
10     following morning the witness and his group joined many other people who
11     were proceeding towards Macedonia.  The police and the VJ escorted the
12     convoy to the border.  At the border the police who was escorting the
13     convoy searched the people and took all their ID, papers, and passports.
14     The witness crossed the border into Macedonia in mid-April 1999.
