13             I will now proceed to read the in-court summary of this witness's
14     evidence.
15             The witness was a driver in a VJ logistic corps.  He describes
16     the combat groups present in Prizren, Suva Reka, Orahovac, and Dragas
17     areas in 1998 and 1999.  He also describes combat operations in which his
18     unit participated in the summer of 1998 and prior to and during the 1999
19     conflict.  In February 1999, the witness was involved in an operation in
20     Ljubizda.  Later that month he took part in a large-scale operation in a
21     village south of Prizren in which around 30 people were killed.
22             On 25 March 1999 the witness's battalion was deployed to a
23     village south of Mamusa in Prizren municipality along with a PJP unit.
24     The soldiers were ordered to make sure that no one was left in the
25     village.  The witness saw soldiers force villagers, including women and
 1     children, out of a home, line them up, and shoot them in a courtyard.  He
 2     estimates that around 50 to 60 civilians were killed in this operation.
 3     The witness also describes how the bodies of some of the victims were
 4     later transported to an unidentified location and buried there.
 5             The witness describes the coordination between VJ and PJP units
 6     during these joint operations.
