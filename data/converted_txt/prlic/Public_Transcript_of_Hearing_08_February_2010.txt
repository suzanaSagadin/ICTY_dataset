 3     floor.
 4             MR. STRINGER:  Thank you and good afternoon, Mr. President.  Good
 5     afternoon, Your Honours, counsel, and everyone else in and around the
 6     courtroom.
 7             I can inform the Trial Chamber that the Prosecution is already in
 8     the process of preparing a response in respect of the Defence -- the
 9     various Defence filings for additional time for the cross-examination of
10     General Petkovic.  So I don't see any difficulties at all in getting that
11     filed within the time-frame that you've just -- you've just set out.
12             The other brief matter I wanted to raise with the -- the Chamber,
13     Mr. President, was that, as you probably know, on the 3rd of February,
14     last week, the Prosecution filed a motion asking for permission to submit
15     a reply, and then we submitted a proposed reply to the Coric Defence's
16     response to the Prosecution's request for additional time to
17     cross-examine the four Coric witnesses who are scheduled to give evidence
18     during March under Rule 92 ter.  And just for the record, Mr. President,
19     we just wanted the Trial Chamber to know that we're aware that the
20     Trial Chamber's practice and preference is to consider whether to grant
21     leave to file a reply before the reply is -- is actually filed, and I
22     think more recently that's -- that's been done when we're in court.  If a
23     party wants to file a reply, they generally rise at some point during the
24     proceedings in court and ask the Trial Chamber for permission to file the
25     reply.
 1             In this situation, because we think that the -- not only now the
 2     Prosecution motion for more time, but there have been other ones filed by
 3     other Defence teams as well for more time to cross-examine these
 4     Rule 92 ter witnesses of the Coric Defence in March, because this is --
 5     could affect the scheduling and the calendar of the various witnesses in
 6     March, we consider it to be a fairly urgent matter to get before the
 7     Trial Chamber as quickly as possible.  And since we were not in court
 8     last week, I elected to go ahead and file this motion to reply as -- as
 9     it is so that we could at least get it in to the Trial Chamber as soon as
10     possible rather than waiting an additional number of days to ask for
11     permission to reply and then to -- if permission was granted to actually
12     file the reply.
13             So we viewed it as -- as something that hopefully would assist
14     the Trial Chamber and the parties and that we could ultimately perhaps
15     get an earlier decision from the Trial Chamber in respect of the
16     cross-examination times for these Rule 92 ter witnesses.  So we simply
17     went ahead and filed the motion to reply with the proposed reply itself,
18     and recognising that's a bit of an irregularity in respect of the
19     Trial Chamber's past practice, but we're nonetheless grateful for the
20     Trial Chamber's consideration on that.  And hopefully we can get the
21     calendar from March formalised as quickly as possible, because it's --
22     obviously everyone needs to know who the witnesses are going to be and
23     how much time's going to be available for the various examinations.
