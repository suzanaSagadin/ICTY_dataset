 2             This Status Conference is called in accordance with Rule 65 bis
 3     (B) of the Rules of Procedure and Evidence.  This rule requires a Status
 4     Conference to be convened within 120 days after the last Status
 5     Conference has been held for each appellant in the Tribunal's custody.
 6             A Status Conference serves two primary purposes:  First, it
 7     allows the appellants an opportunity to express concerns relating to
 8     their appeal or detention conditions and for the Tribunal to inquire
 9     about the mental and physical condition of the detained persons.  Second,
10     it provides an opportunity to update the appellants with respect to the
11     status of the case as well as to inform the general public.
