 4                          --- Upon commencing at 2.15 p.m.
 5            JUDGE ANTONETTI: [Interpretation] Mr. Registrar, will you call the
 6    case, please.
 7            THE REGISTRAR:  Good afternoon, Your Honours.  This is case number
 8    IT-04-74-T, the Prosecutor versus Prlic et al.  Thank you.
 9            JUDGE ANTONETTI: [Interpretation] Thank you, Mr. Registrar.
10            On this Tuesday, the 17th of April, I bid good afternoon to all
11    those present, the representatives of the Prosecution, the ladies and
12    gentlemen of the Defence, as well as the accused.
13            A small correction to the transcript on page 17129 regarding the
14    time allotted to the Defence.  There's an error both in the English and in
15    the French version.  We have decided to give one and a half hours for
16    Mr. Pusic, for Mr. Prlic, and for Mr. Coric, and 50 minutes for the
17    others.  However, in the English version it is stated one and a half hours
18    for Mr. Stojic, which I never said.  And in the French version there's
19    also an error.  It is indicated one and a half hours for Mr. Stojic,
20    whereas it was for Mr. Coric.  Those are the corrections that I had to
21    make.
22            Let me summarise.  One and a half hours for Mr. Prlic, one and a
23    half hours for Mr. Pusic, and one and a half hours for Mr. Coric, and 50
24    minutes for the three others.  That is the correction.
25            Concerning the -- the time taken by the Prosecution, it is one
 1    hour 50.  It would be desirable, Mr. Scott, for you to finish today, which
 2    would roughly make it six hours.  If you finish before that, of course we
 3    will be grateful.
 4            We are now going to put down the blinds before bringing in the
 5    witness, and the hearing will be in closed session.
