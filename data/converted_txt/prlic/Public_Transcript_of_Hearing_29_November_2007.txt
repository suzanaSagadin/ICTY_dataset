 1                          Thursday, 29 November 2007
 2                          [Open session]
 3                          [The accused entered court]
 4                          --- Upon commencing at 9.00 a.m.
 5            JUDGE ANTONETTI: [Interpretation] Madam Registrar, please call the
 6    case.
 7            THE REGISTRAR:  Good morning, Your Honours.  This is case number
 8    IT-04-74-T, the Prosecutor versus Jadranko Prlic et al.
 9            JUDGE ANTONETTI: [Interpretation] Thank you.  Today is Thursday,
10    29th of November.  My greetings to all the people in the courtroom, the
11    Prosecution representatives, the Defence counsel, and the accused.
12            First, I shall give the floor to the registrar, good morning to
13    her, and to all those who assist us.  I need three IC numbers, don't I.
14            THE REGISTRAR:  Your Honours, list of Prosecution exhibits
15    tendered through Witness Amor Masovic will become Exhibit IC 733.
16            Pusic Defence additional request for the admission of documents
17    tendered through Witness Masovic will become Exhibit IC 734.
18            The list of Prosecution exhibits tendered through Witness BC will
19    become Exhibit IC 735, under seal.
20            JUDGE ANTONETTI: [Interpretation] Thank you.  The Trial Chamber is
21    going to hand down two oral decisions.  I shall try to be quick, but these
22    are urgent decisions to be issued.
23            First oral decision:  On the 27th of November, 2007, the
24    Prosecution filed a motion for admission of documents related to Witness
25    EA.  The Trial Chamber recalls that further to two decisions handed down
 1    on the 13th of July and on the 29th of November, 2006, the Prosecution is
 2    allowed to tender documents either through witnesses in court or by
 3    written motion related to a specific municipality or topic.
 4            The Trial Chamber notes that this motion does not meet the
 5    guidelines set forth by the Trial Chamber for admission of documentary
 6    evidence, in that it is not linked to a specific municipality or topic.
 7    Furthermore, the Prosecution has already filed two motions for admission
 8    of evidence linked to Witness EA:  The first time in a written motion
 9    filed on the 5th of July, 2007, and the second time in an oral motion for
10    admission of evidence through Witness EA on the 26th of November, 2007;
11    the IC number being 00722.
12            The evidence thus presented by the Prosecution with regard to
13    Witness EA amounts to some 120 documents.  Given that the Prosecution is
14    under the obligation to select the most important documents and to tender
15    them whilst meeting the guidelines, the Trial Chamber has decided to turn
16    down or dismiss the motion.
17            Second oral decision:  It has to do with a deadline for filing
18    Prosecution motions.
19            The Trial Chamber requests the Prosecution to file any motion by
20    the 10th of December, 2007.  This will enable the Trial Chamber to rule in
21    due time by the end of the Prosecution case scheduled on the 24th of
22    January, 2008.
23            So with regard to the second oral decision, as of the 10th of
24    December, 2007, the Trial Chamber will no longer accept any new motion.
25            Let us move into private session, please.
