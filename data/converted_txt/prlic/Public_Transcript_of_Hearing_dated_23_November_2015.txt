 6             Following an election by the Plenary of Judges of this Tribunal,
 7     I took over the Presidency from Judge Meron on 17 November 2015, and by
 8     operation of the Statute of this Tribunal, I consequently became the
 9     Presiding Judge of these appeal proceedings, whether you like it or not.
10             On 18 November 2015, pursuant to Rules 65 ter and 107 of the
11     Rules of Procedure and Evidence of this Tribunal, I designated myself as
12     Pre-Appeal Judge, and in that capacity, I am conducting the Status
13     Conference today.
14             This Status Conference is called in accordance with
15     Rule 65 bis(B) of the Rules of Procedure and Evidence of this Tribunal.
16     This Rule requires a Status Conference to be convened within 120 days
17     after the last Status Conference has been held for each of the appellants
18     in the Tribunal's custody.
19             By the way, Mr. Ibrisimovic, are you following me?
20             MR. IBRISIMOVIC: [Via videolink] [Interpretation] Yes, I am.
21             JUDGE AGIUS:  Thank you.
22             A Status Conference serves two primary purposes.  First, it
23     allows the appellants an opportunity to express concerns relating to
24     their appeal or detention conditions, and for the Tribunal to inquire
25     about the mental and physical condition of the detained persons.  Second,
 1     it provides an opportunity to update the appellants with respect to the
 2     status of their cases as well as to inform the general public thereof.
 3             I note that the last Status Conference was held on
 4     2nd September of this year, and today's Status Conference was scheduled
 5     by an order issued on the 22nd of October, 2015.

 7     the situation and health of each one of the appellants.
