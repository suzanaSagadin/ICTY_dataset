23            JUDGE LIU:  Mr. Stojanovic, what shall we do?
24            MR. STOJANOVIC: [Interpretation] I have also been caught off guard
25    a little.  Thanks to the spirit of cooperation of the opposing party.  Our
 1    next witness did not ask for any protective measures, Ranko Dzokic, and
 2    because of the arrangements with the VW unit he will be here this
 3    afternoon.  He does not have a direct flight from Belgrade.  He's
 4    travelling via Vienna.  So we will have him here in the courtroom only
 5    tomorrow afternoon.  But with your permission, Your Honours, maybe this is
 6    a good opportunity for me to give you some details about our forthcoming
 7    schedule.
 8            JUDGE LIU:  Yes, of course.  I'm very sorry to hear that we'll
 9    lose at least two sessions of today.  As you know, we are somehow behind
10    schedule, and during this week things -- the Milosevic case is not on, we
11    will have the availability of the courtroom and all the facilities.  But
12    anyway, please brief us about your schedule.
13            MR. STOJANOVIC: [Interpretation] Thank you, Your Honour.  As we
14    have indicated at the beginning of our case, we planned for the following
15    four witnesses, Vukotic, Maric, Bojanovic, and the witness who testified
16    yesterday and today be completed by today.  Tomorrow, we have one witness.
17    On Thursday, we will have two witnesses, Minja Radovic and Brano Djuric,
18    members of the 4th Engineers Company of the Zvornik Brigade.  And on
19    Friday, we will call a witness who is scheduled to testify for the whole
20    day.  So in accordance with the previous list we planned to complete our
21    case with these witnesses which would be fully in accordance with the
22    schedule that we presented earlier to the Court.  All other witnesses will
23    be very brief.  Their testimonies will be quite short and will relate to
24    specific individual counts of the indictment.  Thank you.
25            JUDGE LIU:  Thank you.  Yes, Mr. Karnavas.
 1            MR. KARNAVAS:  With respect to one other witness that was on
 2    Mr. Stojanovic's list of witnesses, as I understand, one of the videolink
 3    witnesses, an agreement has been reached between the Prosecution and
 4    Mr. Stojanovic with respect to one portion of his statement coming in.
 5    And in light of that portion of the statement and in light of the other
 6    witness that will be available via videolink as it would appear, we -- and
 7    in light of the fact that we had already withdrawn this witness from our
 8    witness list, the Court should be aware that we certainly will not be
 9    asking for this witness, that would be Mr. Vasic, to be appearing by
10    videolink.  So that would be a savings of some time as well.
11            JUDGE LIU:  Thank you very much for your cooperation.  Thank you.
12            Yes, Mr. McCloskey.
13            MR. McCLOSKEY:  Just one more issue:  We have spoken to
14    Mr. Stojanovic about his military expert.  And as with Mr. Karnavas's
15    military expert, we will not be objecting to the entry of the report, and
16    I believe Mr. Stojanovic is then agreeing not to call that witness either.
17    So that's another person that has been dealt with.
18            JUDGE LIU:  Thank you very much.  I'm very glad to hear that.  I
19    believe that both parties showed their cooperation with the good faith.
20            We still have time left, so could we come to an ex parte hearing
21    because we want to hear the reports from Mr. Karnavas as well as from
22    Blagojevic himself about the contacts between them and to see what's the
23    result on that.  Do you have any objections, Mr. McCloskey?  I think the
24    best way is to conduct it in private session, ex parte hearing, because it
25    may touch upon the sensitive relationship between the counsel and his
 1    client.
 2            MR. McCLOSKEY:  Yes.  Absolutely, we agree.  So the Prosecution
 3    then will take its leave.
 4            JUDGE LIU:  Thank you very much for your cooperation.
 5            Well, there's a suggestion that maybe the best way is to have a
 6    short break.
 7            Yes, Mr. Stojanovic.

 9    believes that they should perhaps leave the courtroom because of this
10    meeting, we also think that it would perhaps be a good idea for our team
11    to withdraw as well in light of the procedure.  But please, you make the
12    ruling.
