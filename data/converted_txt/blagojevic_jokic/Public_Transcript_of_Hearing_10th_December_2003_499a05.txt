 5            JUDGE LIU:  Yes.  We are now in open session.
 6            MR. McCLOSKEY:  Yes, Mr. President.  We have, well, two witnesses,
 7    a Bratunac Brigade military policeman and a Bosnian army intercept person.
 8    And if anyone has any time or energy left, we have a Dutchman who could
 9    fill in any time or we could save him for January.  So we have perhaps
10    some flexibility.
11            JUDGE LIU:  I see.  Well, I have been just informed that on next
12    Friday, the 19th of December, if we do not finish the witness, we have to
13    sit in the afternoon from 2.00 to 7.00.  I hope that nobody booked a
14    ticket that night.
15            Yes.
16            MR. McCLOSKEY:  We only have two witnesses, Your Honour, in that
17    case.
18            MR. KARNAVAS:  I would prefer, Your Honour, to get as many
19    witnesses done as possible.  I think we can do it, only because, as I have
20    indicated, I think that starting in January it would be -- I understand
21    the Court wants to start on the 15th, but I think it would be wiser to
22    work as much as we can next week and maybe have that entire week off for a
23    couple of reasons, including, one, that I believe Mr. Stojanovic will be
24    with a new co-counsel, and I'm sure he would appreciate the extra time for
25    his co-counsel to come up to speed to be ready when we restart.  So -- but
 1    in any event I certainly don't want to sit on Friday if we don't have to,
 2    but I'm prepared.  I don't have a ticket and I'm not leaving town for a
 3    while, so it doesn't matter to me.
 4            JUDGE LIU:  Well, I think having said that, we'll resume next
 5    Tuesday, 2.15, in Courtroom I.  The hearing is adjourned.
