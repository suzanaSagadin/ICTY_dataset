 2                           [Open session]
 3                           --- Upon commencing at 2.19 p.m. 
 4                           [The accused entered court]
 5             JUDGE ANTONETTI: [Interpretation] Mr. Registrar, could you call 
 6     the case, please.  
 7             THE REGISTRAR:  Your Honours, case number IT-01-47-T, the 
 8     Prosecutor versus Enver Hadzihasanovic and Amir Kubura. 
 9             JUDGE ANTONETTI: [Interpretation] Thank you, Mr. Registrar.  
10     Could we have the appearances for the Prosecution.
11             MR. MUNDIS:  Good afternoon, Mr. President, Your Honours, 
12     Counsel, and everyone in and around the courtroom.  For the Prosecution, 
13     Tecla Benjamin-Henry, Daryl Mundis, and the case manager, Mr. Andres 
14     Vatter. 
15             JUDGE ANTONETTI: [Interpretation] Thank you, Mr. Mundis.  Could 
16     we have the appearances for Defence counsel.  
17             MS. RESIDOVIC: [Interpretation] Good day, Mr. President.  Good 
18     day, Your Honours.  On behalf of General Enver Hadzihasanovic, Edina 
19     Residovic, counsel; Stephane Bourgon, co-counsel; and Muriel Cauvin, our 
20     legal assistant.  Thank you. 
21             MR. IBRISIMOVIC: [Interpretation] Good day, Your Honours.  On 
22     behalf of Mr. Kubura, Rodney Dixon, Fahrudin Ibrisimovic, and Nermin  
23     Mulalic, our legal assistant. 
24             JUDGE ANTONETTI: [Interpretation] Thank you.  The Trial Chamber 
25     would like to greet everyone present, the accused, members of the 
 1     Prosecution, and Ms. Benjamin in particular who is back with us in the 
 2     courtroom.  Naturally, I would like to greet Defence counsel and  
 3     Ms. Cauvin, whom we haven't seen for a while.  We would also like to 
 4     greet the accused and everyone else in the courtroom. 
 5             As you know, we will be examining a witness today.  But before we 
 6     call the witness into the courtroom, I would like to inform the parties  
 7     that the Prosecution on the 10th of June filed a request with the Trial  
 8     Chamber in order to admit a written statement pursuant to Rule 98 bis.  
 9     This concerns a number of issues in connection to potential witnesses who 
10     are now deceased. 
11             I would like the Defence to briefly comment on this request -- on 
12     this motion, so that we can rule on the motion as soon as possible.  I 
13     should point out that as you are aware - the Trial Chamber has already  
14     indicated this - we hope that the Prosecution's case will be concluded by  
15     the end of June, and we hope that the Appeal Chamber will also have  
16     rendered its decision about a witness for whom protective measures have 
17     been requested. 
18             I'm now going to ask the registrar to go into private session.  
19     And could you check that there is no one in the gallery, unless we lower  
20     the blinds.  I can't see anything from where I'm seated.
