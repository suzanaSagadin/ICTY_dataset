10             JUDGE ORIE:  Thank you, Madam Registrar.
11             For the sake of transparency, the Chamber would like to put on
12     the record what was done in preparation for the testimonies -- testimony
13     of the Chamber witness, number 3.
14             The steps taken, with regard to calling Witness CW-3 were the
15     following.  On the 26th of February, 2010, a legal officer of the Chamber
16     contacted Witness CW-3 by phone and informed him about the Chamber's
17     decision and about the possible date for testimony.  This telephone
18     conversation was recorded, with the knowledge of the witness, and may be
19     provided to the parties should any dispute arise about what was said
20     during this telephone conversation.
21             I would also like to put on the record that, on the 3rd of March,
22     the Chamber granted the Prosecution's request to send a Request for
23     Assistance to Croatia for Witness CW-3, to the extent that the Request
24     for Assistance did not require Croatia to contact Witness CW-3 regarding
25     the substance of his testimony.
