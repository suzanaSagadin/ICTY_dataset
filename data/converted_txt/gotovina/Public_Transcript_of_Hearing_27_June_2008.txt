13             I started reading a decision on the admission of MFI D191, and I
14     summarized the portion that I had read, that is, that a list of members
15     of the 113th Brigade on which it was suggested the name of the witness
16     Gojanovic did not appear, was so long that the Chamber sought a shorter
17     version to be submitted into evidence.  The Chamber also wanted to check
18     for itself whether the witness was listed in the document, and on the
19     11th of June, the parties informed the Chamber that they had agreed that
20     D191 should be comprised only of the first two pages and the last page,
21     which bears a signature.
22             For the relevant transcript pages relating to this procedural
23     history, see the pages 3014 to 3016 and 4818 to 4819.
24             The Chamber has also checked the document and admits it into
25     evidence in its shortened version.  The Gotovina Defence is invited, if
 1     it has not already done so, to upload the two first pages and the last
 2     page of the current version of D191 and then to notify the registrar.
