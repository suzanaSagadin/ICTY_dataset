 1            JUDGE ROBINSON:  Is there any particular matter that you wish to
 2    raise in relation to the trial, or has your attorney spoken for you?
 3            THE ACCUSED: [Interpretation] As far as the witnesses, I would
 4    like to add what I've already stated during an interview to the
 5    investigator of The Hague Tribunal, during the interview in Podgorica, and
 6    that is that the information about the identity of the protected witness
 7    was something that was known to the entire municipality of Plav one day
 8    before it was published in the newspaper, and we can bring as many
 9    witnesses as the Court believes it requires in order to confirm what I
10    have just stated.
11            I also believe that the protected witness himself would be able to
12    repeat what he has already said during a protected interview for a
13    magazine which is published in Montenegro.  It's a weekly magazine in
14    which he confirmed that at the point when he testified, his voice was
15    already recognised in Plav as his, as his authentic voice, and his
16    identity was already known in Plav, it was known that he was the one who
17    was testifying.
18            The other thing I would like to say is that if my previous
19    statement about my guilt or non-guilt has given rise to some uncertainties
20    or unclarified things, I would like to explain it further.  I said that I
21    do not deny my objective responsibility as the editor-in-chief of the
22    newspaper.  And I take that responsibility, but I do not recognise that
23    there is any subjective responsibility in revealing the identity of the
24    witness or in any subjective responsibility as relating to whether I lied
25    before The Hague Tribunal or not, or that I was in contempt of the
 1    Tribunal.
