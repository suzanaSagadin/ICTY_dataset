 1                          Friday, 5 December 2003
 2                          [Rule 77 Hearing - Initial Appearance]
 3                          [The accused entered court]
 4                          --- Upon commencing at 2.00 p.m.
 5            JUDGE ANTONETTI: [Interpretation] I would like to ask
 6    Madam Registrar to call the case, please.
 7            THE REGISTRAR:  Good afternoon, Your Honour.  This is case number
 8    IT-02-54-T-R77.2, the Prosecutor versus Dusko Jovanovic.
 9            JUDGE ANTONETTI: [Interpretation] I shall now ask the Prosecution
10    to introduce himself or herself.
11            MS. ROMANO:  Your Honour, Cristina Romano for the Prosecution, and
12    with me is Milbert Shin and our case manager, Susan Grogan.
13            JUDGE ANTONETTI: [Interpretation] Thank you.  I would like to ask
14    the attorney of the accused to introduce himself, please.
15            MR. SIMIC: [Interpretation] Your Honour, my name is Sinisa Simic.
16    I'm attorney from Belgrade, Defence counsel for the accused
17    Dusko Jovanovic, editor-in-chief for the Montenegrin newspaper DAN.
18            JUDGE ANTONETTI: [Interpretation] Thank you.  I shall now ask the
19    accused to stand up, please.
20            Could you give me your first name and second name, please.  And
21    last name, sorry.
22            THE ACCUSED: [Interpretation] Your Honour, my name is
23    Dusko Jovanovic.
24            JUDGE ANTONETTI: [Interpretation] When were you born?
25            THE ACCUSED: [Interpretation] On the 2nd of February, 1964, in
 1    Podgorica.
 2            JUDGE ANTONETTI: [Interpretation] What is your job?
 3            THE ACCUSED: [Interpretation] I have a degree in law, and now I am
 4    editor-in-chief of the weekly DAN.
 5            JUDGE ANTONETTI: [Interpretation] Where do you live?
 6            THE ACCUSED: [Interpretation] Podgorica, Montenegro.
 7            JUDGE ANTONETTI: [Interpretation] Thank you.  Have you -- has the
 8    indictment been served on you and were you able to read this indictment in
 9    your own native language?
10            THE ACCUSED: [Interpretation] Yes, Your Honour, I have received
11    the indictment, and I have read it.
12            JUDGE ANTONETTI: [Interpretation] Did you understand the contents
13    thereof?
14            THE ACCUSED: [Interpretation] I have understood the contents.
15            JUDGE ANTONETTI: [Interpretation] Would you like the indictment to
16    be read out to you or do you feel that you know what this indictment
17    contains to the full?
18            THE ACCUSED: [Interpretation] There's no need for it to be read
19    out.  I have understood it fully.
20            JUDGE ANTONETTI: [Interpretation] Thank you.  Pursuant to
21    Article 62 of the Rules of Procedure and Evidence, I have the obligation
22    to check that you have an attorney to defend you, which is the case.  As
23    regards the indictment, you told me that you had read it.  I must also let
24    you know that within 30 days of the initial appearance, i.e. today, you
25    will be asked whether you want to plead guilty or not guilty, but you can,
 1    if you so wish, plead guilty or not guilty today on the count you are
 2    charged with.
 3            Could you let me know what your position is, please?  Would you
 4    like to settle for the 30-day delay or would you like to enter a plea
 5    today and state whether you enter a plea of guilty or not guilty?
 6            THE ACCUSED: [Interpretation] Your Honour, I think I could enter a
 7    plea today already as to whether I feel guilty or not guilty.
 8            JUDGE ANTONETTI: [Interpretation] And what do you decide to do?
 9            THE ACCUSED: [Interpretation] I don't think it is so simple that I
10    can answer with a guilty or not guilty.  I think that in my case, there is
11    objective responsibility because of my position as director and
12    editor-in-chief of DAN, but no guilt in the intention to show contempt for
13    the Court or to hurt a protected witness.  And in order to explain the
14    reasons, this would take a little more time, and I would need to consult
15    with my counsel.
16            JUDGE ANTONETTI: [Interpretation] Fine.  I shall draw the
17    following conclusion:  You plead neither guilty nor not guilty today.  It
18    is -- we are talking now about a plea of not guilty consequently, and
19    pursuant to the Rules of Procedure and Evidence, pursuant to Article 62
20    bis, you have 30 days in which to stipulate your position.
21            As you undoubtedly know, this case will come under the
22    jurisdiction of Chamber III, and you will be notified in the next few days
23    by the Registry of the date which will be set to start the proceedings
24    pursuant to Article 62 bis.  As we are today the 5th of December, 30 days
25    from now, that will be the 5th of January.  This Tribunal, however, only
 1    resumes on the 12th of January.  Therefore, the date we will set for the
 2    said proceedings will be after the 12th of January.
 3            Does the Prosecution at this stage of the hearing have any
 4    arguments to submit?
 5            MS. ROMANO:  No, Your Honour.
 6            JUDGE ANTONETTI: [Interpretation] Fine.  I shall now give the
 7    floor to the attorney of the accused.  Sir, do you have any comments to
 8    make?
 9            MR. SIMIC: [Interpretation] Your Honour, I would just like on
10    behalf of my client, Mr. Dusko Jovanovic, to apologise for not appearing
11    here yesterday.  This was due to objective circumstances.  We had not
12    received a visa from the Kingdom of the Netherlands in due time, and that
13    is why we did not appear yesterday, and we owe you an apology for this.
14            We should also like, pursuant to the Rules of this Tribunal, to
15    receive the material relating to the indictment presented today, the
16    supporting material, in other words.  Thank you.
17            JUDGE ANTONETTI: [Interpretation] Fine.  I shall now turn to the
18    Prosecution.  Pursuant to Article 66 of the Rules of Procedure and
19    Evidence, the Prosecution has the obligation to send within 30 days as of
20    the date of the initial appearance copies of all the supporting material
21    as well as any other documents.  Can you confirm that this will be done in
22    the given time frame?
23            MS. ROMANO:  Your Honour, the supporting material is available
24    immediately for the accused and the Defence, so we believe that right
25    after the hearing we are ready to deliver.
 1            JUDGE ANTONETTI: [Interpretation] Very well.  The Defence has just
 2    been informed of the fact that any documents will be handed over to them.
 3            Do you have any further questions?  If not, I would like to thank
 4    all the parties present here today, and I declare this initial appearance
 5    hearing adjourned.  And the Registry will let the Defence know about the
 6    next hearing date.
 7                          --- Whereupon the Rule 77 Hearing -
 8                          Initial Appearance adjourned at 2.11 p.m.
