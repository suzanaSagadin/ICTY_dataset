# ICTY Case Information Sheets — Report

*Prepared by Suzana Sagadin and Claude (Anthropic)*

> **Note on data quality:** This report was generated by automated parsing of 69 Case Information
> Sheet PDFs from the ICTY archive. The PDFs come in at least 4 different formats (spanning
> ~20 years of tribunal activity), so the extraction is not complete or fully consistent across
> all cases. Some fields may be missing, misattributed, or imprecise. This report is meant to
> give a first impression of the dataset and help with corpus selection — not to serve as a
> definitive reference. Always verify against the original PDFs for authoritative data.

**Cases parsed:** 69 (out of 86 case folders in the archive)
**Source:** Case Information Sheet PDFs from the ICTY archive

The 17 missing cases break down as: 7 are duplicate/legacy folders that map to cases already
parsed under their main slug (e.g. `blagojevic_old` → `blagojevic_jokic`), 1 is a `_statistics`
folder (not a real case), and 9 are co-defendants whose information is covered by the joint
case sheet of their parent trial (e.g. Beara and Borovčanin are part of Popović et al.).

## Pleas

Per accused (116 accused across the 69 cases, see `pleas.csv` — one row per
accused, extracted by `parser/extract_pleas.py`). The `plea` column records
the **initial** plea; later changes are recorded in the `note` column:

| Initial plea | Accused |
|---|---|
| not guilty | 111 |
| guilty | 2 |
| unknown | 3 |

Of the 111 who initially pleaded not guilty, 15 later changed their plea to
guilty (noted per row), and 1 (Kunarac) had first entered a guilty plea to a
single count and withdrew it — so 17 accused eventually pleaded guilty. The
3 unknowns never entered a plea (died before trial / indictment withdrawn).
The case-level `plea` field in the JSON/CSV files is less reliable: in joint
cases and case narratives it can pick up co-accused's or co-indictees' plea
agreements.
